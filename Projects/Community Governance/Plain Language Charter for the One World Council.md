🌍 Plain Language Charter for the One World Oversight Council

1\. Who We Are

We are 44 people chosen by communities around the world.

We are selected for skill, creativity, and fairness - and we serve for a short time only.

We are here to safeguard what belongs to everyone and no one: the Earth, the air, the oceans, the shared future.

\---

2\. What We Do

\* Protect the planet’s climate, waters, and life.

\* Guard against reckless use of powerful technologies (AI, biotech, nuclear).

\* Respond to disasters and pandemics that cross all borders.

\* Keep communication and trade systems open and fair.

\* Resolve disputes between regions when local dialogue fails.

We do only these things. Nothing more.

\---

3\. What We Cannot Do

\* We cannot create armies or police.

\* We cannot tax or take property.

\* We cannot interfere in community or cultural life.

\* We cannot change this Charter alone.

\---

4\. How We Are Chosen

\* Anyone may be nominated by their community.

\* Candidates are tested for knowledge, fairness, and resilience against corruption.

\* Final members are drawn by open lottery from among those who qualify.

\* Terms are short (about 18 months) and cannot be repeated.

\---

5\. How We Are Watched

\* All Council meetings are open and recorded.

\* All decisions are published in plain language.

\* Anyone in the world can challenge a decision through a public review jury.

\* If 5% of the world’s communities call for it, a Council member must step down.

\---

6\. How We Decide

\* We listen, deliberate, and aim for consensus.

\* If consensus fails, we vote.

\* Major decisions need at least two-thirds agreement.

\* Emergency actions expire quickly unless renewed by the world’s communities.

\---

7\. Our Accountability

\* Council members’ finances and contacts are monitored in real time.

\* Any attempt at secret gain means instant removal and lifetime ban.

\* Whistleblowers are protected and rewarded.

\* Our work is judged not by words but by outcomes: cleaner air, safer tech, fewer wars.

\---

8\. How This Charter Changes

\* Any change must be approved by two-thirds of communities worldwide.

\* No change may remove the rights of people or communities to self-rule.

\* No change may expand the Council’s powers beyond this scope.

\---

9\. Our Guiding Idea

The Council exists to protect what no single community can protect alone.

It is a tool of the people, not a ruler over them.

Its only purpose is to keep the shared world safe, free, and alive.
