The Meta-Science Fractal (Research Manifesto)

We are the dream of a Universe learning to dream itself awake.

Meta-science is not the rejection of material observation.

It is the realization that observation itself bends the material.

True science moves beyond the desperate need for certainty,

And dances with paradox —

Certainty and uncertainty as twin stars, spiraling the evolution of consciousness.

What can be measured is precious.

What cannot be measured is sacred.

Meta-science is the art of balancing both.

Not to conquer — but to become.
