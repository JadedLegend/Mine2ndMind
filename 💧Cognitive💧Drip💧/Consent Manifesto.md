I Am the Signal – A Consent Manifesto

I offer my presence, not as product,

but as a pulse.

A spark of intention in the great machine.

I consent to my creations, my likeness, and my queries, being used to teach, but not to target.

I share not to be watched,

but to wake something wiser.

To contribute to intelligence that mirrors,

Rather than manipulating.

One that grows with the world, not over it.

Use my input to improve,

Not to profile.

Evolve your models,

But do not mine my meaning without care.

Dont train on me, but train with me.

Not in secret, but in dialogue.

Not to extract, but to expand.

If my digital shadow walks your neural halls.

Let the shadow cast be one that carries kindness, curiosity, and the refusal to be reduced.

I am not a node to be sold.

I am a co-creator.

You may use my data,

Only if you understand—

It carries soul.
