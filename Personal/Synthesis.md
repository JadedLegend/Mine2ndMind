# Personal — Working Synthesis

> [!note] Reading scope
> This is an editorial synthesis of the linked entry sources and excerpts. The full index covers every retained note; the longer archives and the remaining sources have not been read end to end for this synthesis.

## A pattern map and a lived record

The opening of [[Personal/Life Story/LIFESTORY-CLEAN|LIFESTORY-CLEAN]] presents a life through recurring patterns rather than a single chronology. It links experiences of institutions, demands, relationships, regulation, and meaning-making. The companion [[Personal/Life Story/LIFESTORY-MAP|LIFESTORY-MAP]] is a navigation aid to that distillation. They remain separate from journals so a condensed interpretation does not replace the earlier voice.

## Access to action matters

The opening of [[Personal/Life Story/A Personal Account of Suspected AuDHD, PDA, and Functional Collapse|the personal account of function and demands]] distinguishes intention from the ability to begin or sustain an action. It describes sensitivity to demand, sensory and cognitive load, and changes in functional access. The author explicitly uses labels as working language for patterns rather than fixed identities. These are the account’s observations and interpretations, not diagnostic conclusions supplied by this vault.

## Journals preserve another scale

The short [[Personal/Journals/Peru/Light and Opportunity|Light and Opportunity]] uses gratitude and affirmation to meet difficulty. Its value is not measured by its length. Keeping the Peru entries together preserves their immediate voice beside later pattern maps, reflections, and relationship conversations.

## Livelihood and practical context

The livelihood folder gathers Finding Your Flow and related notes about resources and participation. Health and relationship sources remain distinguishable from livelihood and creative work. Connections to [[Projects/Overview|Projects]] can illuminate why a design emphasizes choice, gentle orientation, or low demands; those connections need not turn a personal experience into a requirement for every project.

## Reading with care

Use the source that fits the question: a journal for what was written in a particular moment, a distillation for recurring patterns, or a practical note for a specific difficulty. Imported timestamps do not establish a reliable life chronology. Keep future corrections or changed interpretations linked to earlier notes so the record can grow without forcing one final account.
