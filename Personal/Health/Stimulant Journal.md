⚖️ 20 mg — Sub-threshold

You were getting partial catecholamine engagement: enough to lift sluggishness and start cognitive momentum, but not enough to fully stabilize dopamine tone.

You likely felt “almost there,” needing external push or novelty to maintain focus.

➡️ Useful for low-demand or recovery days.

—

⚙️ 30 mg — Functional window

That’s your therapeutic zone.

Executive function is available.

Tasks feel neutral (not forced or euphoric).

Your body doesn’t buzz or tighten.

That’s the “regulation” range — the dose where your dopamine circuits are supported, not driven.

—

⚡ 40 mg — Reward threshold

Here you cross from regulation → reinforcement.

You feel euphoric because dopamine in nucleus accumbens (reward center) exceeds the prefrontal optimal zone.

It feels great short-term, but long-term it risks:

Faster tolerance build-up

Emotional rebound / irritability later in the day

Autonomic strain (heart rate, tension, temperature swings)
