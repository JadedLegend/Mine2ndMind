How to run it (4-week map)

Weeks 1–2 — Quiet the noise, restore the signal

AM: PEA (600 mg), Omega-3, Lion’s Mane, (L-Threonate if using in AM), BCP (tiny dose if you like it day-calm).

PM: PEA (600 mg), Magnesium Glycinate, L-Theanine.

Daily gut training: Psyllium in water (separate from meds/supps by ~2h), walk after meals, sit 10–15 min after breakfast (gastro-colic reflex = built-in “go” signal).

If backed up: Use Magnesium Citrate once in week 1 (then back to daily psyllium + glycinate).

Weeks 3–4 — Rebuild rhythm, add clarity

Keep PEA steady; evaluate if 600 mg/day is enough.

Keep Omega-3 and Glycinate nightly; L-Threonate earlier in day for brain clarity.

Lion’s Mane stays daily; if energy feels flat, add gentle ginger tea with meals (pro-motility).

BCP 3–5 days/week (not daily) to avoid blunting your own sensing.

\> If sleep is still choppy: bump theanine to 300 mg or add glycine (3 g) at night (cheap, effective).

\---

Guardrails & notes

Constipation history (decades): keep your clinician looped in; consider motility workup if you’ve never had one. Use citrate as rescue, not daily. Train daily flow with fiber + hydration + timing.

Medication interactions: magnesium can chelate some meds (separate by 2–4h).

Stack minimalism: add new pieces one at a time every 3–4 days so you can feel what moves the needle.
