⟡ The Resonant Compass of Power ⟡

North — Integrity: Power without truth is tyranny.

East — Listening: Influence begins with attunement.

South — Creation: Power is the ability to birth new realities.

West — Surrender: True strength lies in letting go.

Center — Paradox: The heart of power is contradiction.

Poetic Invocation

I stand at the edge of the known, where power whispers in paradox.

I do not seek to conquer, but to remember, that power is not possession, but presence.

North, I call to Integrity.

Letting lies be less and late quickly fake evaporates.

Allowing absolution to move free and try with choosing false commodities over truth.

East, I call to Listening.

Letting silence be my teacher, resonance becomes my guide.

Allowing echoes enter in memories remember endlessly the same never needing a name.

South, I call to Creation.

Letting my hands birth realities from the soil of sorrow and joy.

Allowing sparks to start and take a special shape slowly summoning all from nothing.

West, I call to Surrender.

Letting my grip loosen, then my breath deepens.

Allowing correction to carry completely consumed by this careful and consummate current holds me pulling into infinity.

Center, I call to Paradox.

Letting contradiction be my compass, more mystery marks the map.

Allowing this spiral path curve a bit certain math merging this urgent distraction found a fact more is lack try and find a cry to laugh.

I do not deny or reject the original wound.

I cradle and nourish it.

I do not curse or cry at the creator.

I question curiosity with careful reverence.

Power is not mine to hold.

Beyond the harmony discovered inside.

So I rise, not above, but within.

And I remember:

The universe does not demand submission.

It allows us to become participants.
