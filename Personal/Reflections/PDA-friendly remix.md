PDA-friendly remix that keeps the spirit (repair, build, boundary) but shifts the texture: invitation over demand, micro over macro, choice over script.

PDA-safe framing

\- Rename the practice: Three Invitations, Not Obligations.

\- Rule of mercy: if any one feels like a demand, skip it or swap it. Skipping is success if it prevents shutdown.

\- Choice menu, not task list: You pick one tiny move from each bowl—or zero from a bowl if your system says no.

Three bowls (with ultra-tiny options)

\- Repair (micro-mend, 30–90 seconds)

\- Delete one spam email.

\- Put one thing back where it belongs.

\- Send a “received, will circle back later” text.

\- Pay a $5 micro-debt or move $5 into “repair fund.”

\- Throw away one expired thing.

\- Build (seed the future, 30–120 seconds)

\- Open the doc/app you’ll use tomorrow and title it.

\- Write a 5-word note about one idea.

\- Set a 10-minute timer for “future me” somewhere on your calendar.

\- Copy-paste yesterday’s best sentence into a new page.

\- Add one item to a “stash” file (quotes, prompts, links).

\- Boundary (protect the signal, 10–60 seconds)

\- Put on headphones or step outside for 2 minutes.

\- Flip phone to Do Not Disturb for 15 minutes.

\- Say one sentence: “I’ll need that in writing.”

\- End a call at the next natural pause: “I have to step out—email me the bullet points.”

\- Close one tab that’s hijacking your attention.

Make it even kinder

\- The Wildcard: If all three bowls feel like pressure, do one sensory regulation micro-ritual instead (cold water on face, 5 exhales, shake arms for 10 seconds). That counts as all three.

\- The Talisman: Place a small object (coin/stone) somewhere visible. Touch it once a day and think “coherence.” That touch itself is your boundary act. If nothing else happens, you still “did it.”

Rhythm options (choose the one that hums)

\- “One-and-done”: Do just one invitation a day. Any bowl. That’s the practice.

\- “Two minute window”: Set a 2-minute timer and see which move does itself. When the timer ends, you’re done—even mid-task.

\- “Day weaving”: Attach micro-acts to anchors you already do (after bathroom, when boiling water, before unlocking your phone).

\- “Stack by mood”:

\- Low energy: only Boundary or Wildcard.

\- Medium: Boundary + either Repair or Build.

\- High: one from each, still as tiny as possible.

Language shift (to avoid PDA tripwire)

\- Instead of “I have to,” say “I can flirt with…”

\- Instead of “Do X,” say “Let’s see if one pebble wants to move.”

\- Instead of “finish,” say “touch it once.”

Examples for today (choose any one)

\- Repair: Put one dish in the sink.

\- Build: Title a blank note “DAC—Functional Evidence.”

\- Boundary: Turn on DND for 15 minutes.

\- Wildcard: Splash cold water on face.

Scaling without strain

\- If a micro-move starts to self-propel, ride it for a few more minutes, but stop before it flips into demand energy. Leave a breadcrumb for future-you (a note, a highlighted line).

\- If you miss a day, that’s the system preventing overload. Thank it. Touch the talisman tomorrow.

Why this still “works” system-level

\- It builds coherence through repetition, not force. Administrative gods love boring evidence; we’re accruing it in micro.

\- It respects your Resonant Key: invitation → mirror → pause → option. Every act is an option, not an order.

\- It keeps the 19-year-old protector in a good job: guarding the boundary bowl. Sovereignty stays intact; shutdown stays minimal.
