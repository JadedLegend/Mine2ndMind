"A man is rich in proportion to the number of things which he can afford to leave alone."

The paradoxical nature of reality. Such a delicate line one must walk to live a balanced life.

Unless I can do without something, it owns me.

The attachment to people, places, and things can feel like a very comfortable satisfaction.

One can’t be truly comfortable with themselves until they’re at the very least willing to be completely by themselves.

Wealth has nothing to do with money.

It does manifest with money sometimes,

but it’s an energy.

The energy of abundance. Known it.

Have known it—regardless of the material objects in my life.

When the intentions and energies work against each other instead of together toward a common goal, blockages develop and the energy has placed numerous blockages which I am prepared to break down and move through.

I wish to destroy them all with one fell swoop, but deeply see the impossibility of this all-at-once possibility.

I reach for a cathartic release of pent-up aggression which has buried itself deep within passivity.

I search for a flow state which can be monetized in hopes of finding magical freedom.

To become a billionaire for me means positively affecting the lives of a billion people.

This is my goal. The unobtainablility of it all makes it seem so near

Where do I start? Right here.

And now.

Realize that I don’t need to find anything—

I simply need to recognize my internal wealth

and express it.

This project to amass and archive this

piece of paper is a humble beginning.

Rip it. Burn it. Throw it out. Give it away.

Whoever you do,

Whenever you do,

Realize it's not yours.

Yet it is you.
