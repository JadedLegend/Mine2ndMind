I have been living in an unfounded fear for so long that it seemed normal. My physical and mental constructs have been manipulated from many angles. My self believes it is serving, but for what? In actuality it is preventing me from really living.

Trapped in a perpetuating cycle of fear and pain. Blocking my connection to anything beyond what I can see. And preventing me from being able to truly appreciate the beauty that surrounds me.

Tears of joy, magnificent paradox. Emotions and sensations which have no names. Occasionally distracting me. But I refuse to submit to the ornament, instead focusing on the here and now.

Appreciating the majesty of an unreal reality. Recognizing the love of hate. The beauty of pain. Crazy in awe at the power of duality. Two opposing forces which power each other. Working in conjunction in a way which is impossible. Impossible. Such a contradictory world.
