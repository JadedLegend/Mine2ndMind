A perfect storm.

I wish to sit in the eye of it for all infinity.

But all infinity is now—

That’s really all there is.

I’ve got a lot to give,

If you’d only give a bit of time.

But what is time?

It’s just illusion.

Always here and never moving.

The truth is really just a lie.

I love it really, when I cry.

Separate manifestations of a single mind

might try and fight against one another…

just love your sisters and your brothers.

I truly feel—

Compacted fractures of attraction

have regrouped in the fraction divine.

Math and God is laughing:

How oh how did this all happen?

To love.

To love.
