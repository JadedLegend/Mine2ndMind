### **Balance and Flow**

Every decision I make is important.  
My main goal has to be to continue opening all of these energy pathways.

But due to the scope of the game,  
I do need and want some distractions.

Keeping those distractions balanced  
and energy flowing is crucial.

I’m aiming for progress, not perfection.  
Instead of forcing things,  
I need to let them take shape on their own,  
with my intuition guiding them —  
crafting the most comfortable reality I can.

Taking my time, but not taking too much of it.  
Settling into the flow.
