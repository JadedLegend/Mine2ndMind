### **Planning and Progress**

How defined of a plan should I have?  
Should I plan out my steps and be open to changes?  
Or should I have a general outline which I can work around?

I think the most important piece may be my daily routine —  
finding one that leaves me feeling fulfilled but not overwhelmed.

I also think it’s important to stop non-serving patterns,  
to be aware of every decision I make,  
be aware that I’ll never attain perfection,  
so learn to accept the imperfection  
but strive for progress.
