## **🌿 *Amazon Journal — Peru, 2013 (Complete Transcription)***

### **Internal Ceremony**

Last night while trying to sleep, I had another internal ceremony.  
I also had a similar experience the previous night, but this was much more pronounced.

A lot of tossing and turning — and I remember having the conscious thought of,  
*“When is this ceremony going to be over?”  
*a few times, then relaxing back into it.

When I woke up this morning I felt great.  
I’m not supposed to do any insulin today and tomorrow,  
and regardless of how much I trust, I—

### **Nü Nü Ceremony**

Last night during the Nü Nü ceremony, I was half asleep the whole time,  
going through my own internal ceremony.

Much like an ayahuasca ceremony — it’s hard to put to words.  
It was uncomfortable. I was sweating a lot,  
my skin was crawling, and I felt myself purge with everyone else’s various purges.

When I woke up and was leaving the room to check my blood sugar,  
the ceremony had ended.

When I went to check my blood sugar, it was the highest it’s been since I’ve been here —  
which is nonsensical based on the things I had eaten, scientifically at least.

### **Intuition and Paradox**

My intuition has never felt so misleading and paradoxical before.  
A major theme, which I do somewhat trust,  
is that my personal universe is for me — to be careful what I share.

But my intuition also says that I need help figuring out this sorcery  
that cannot be figured out.

I feel the counterpart to every point;  
both seem just as valid.  
I wonder how to make the switch from opposing forces  
to oneness and neutrality.

It’s obviously a multifaceted answer —  
which isn’t even an answer really,  
just another question.  
So I asked...

### **Planning and Progress**

How defined of a plan should I have?  
Should I plan out my steps and be open to changes?  
Or should I have a general outline which I can work around?

I think the most important piece may be my daily routine —  
finding one that leaves me feeling fulfilled but not overwhelmed.

I also think it’s important to stop non-serving patterns,  
to be aware of every decision I make,  
be aware that I’ll never attain perfection,  
so learn to accept the imperfection  
but strive for progress.

### **Balance and Flow**

Every decision I make is important.  
My main goal has to be to continue opening all of these energy pathways.

But due to the scope of the game,  
I do need and want some distractions.

Keeping those distractions balanced  
and energy flowing is crucial.

I’m aiming for progress, not perfection.  
Instead of forcing things,  
I need to let them take shape on their own,  
with my intuition guiding them —  
crafting the most comfortable reality I can.

Taking my time, but not taking too much of it.  
Settling into the flow.

### **Darkness and Understanding**

So many more questions.  
I see my darkness more clearly.  
Although it’s still quite blurry,  
it feels like a huge enemy and challenge.

But my realization from yesterday keeps showing up:  
my greatest enemy is my best friend.

I do wish that I had some more concrete ideas  
instead of so much abstraction,  
but I guess that’s how the game is played.

I come looking for answers,  
get them all and then some,  
but keep uncovering new levels of questions.

### **Gratitude and Healing**

Peru was the best trip I’ve ever had.  
Words can’t express my gratitude for being able to go on this trip.

All of the friends I made are so amazing,  
and I’m going to see most of them again.

The medicine cleared out so many of my blockages,  
which I had been struggling with.

I feel clear-headed, balanced, happy, grateful,  
and I clearly see the path I want to take.

It doesn’t need to be fully figured out —  
just aligned.

### **Action and Alignment**

To figure out some little semblance of an action plan —  
to work towards and with the divine plan  
while in a human body, instead of fighting it.

I’m really ready at this point,  
but all I can care for now  
is healing my own personal body, heart, and soul,  
and hopefully the rest will follow.

### **Technology and Inner Tools**

Reliance on other technology.  
So my computer’s broken, and for some reason,  
I feel this strange peace about it.

It’s part of my transition — my lesson.  
This experience has shown me  
how reliant upon technology I am.

I feel as though technology is only a shortcut,  
a manifestation of our own inner technologies —  
more than a replacement.

Outer technology is only the incomplete manifestation  
of our inner technology.

### **Polarization and Integration**

Trying to find a balance.  
It feels important to me  
not to become polarized to the light or the dark,  
but instead to stay neutral.

I feel as if a polarization towards the light  
can cause a comfortably harsh imbalance.

Also, if the light and the dark  
are held in the same container,  
why would we want to eradicate either of them?

But where does the physical integration of this principle lie?  
Because that’s what I’m interested in doing —  
grounding these unfathomable energies  
in a way which can help us in this physical plane.

### **Vision and Routine**

Ok, I think it’s a good idea to put down how I’d like my life to be.  
I wake up around 8am in the morning.

I make coffee, I do a quick morning stretch,  
I meditate for 15 minutes.

I make breakfast and drink some coffee.  
I eat, then brush my teeth and take my supplements.

I sit down to write for at least an hour —  
whether music or something else.

I focus on being healthy and my body feels good and comfortable.

I eat healthy, I exercise, I do yoga.

I have an amazing group of friends who I love  
and who love me.

I pursue whatever avenues I feel I should —  
which are presented to me.

I cook, I create, I’m happy, joyous, and free.  
I live in California and maybe Oregon.  
Make money on the side with weed  
in a balanced, legal manner.

### **Ceremony and Revelation**

Every ceremony, more is revealed.  
I (we) are creating this universe.

There are all sorts of “negative” thought forms  
that want to take away from the beauty  
that humans are capable of for their own gains.

I’m not sure if it’s ever been done before or not,  
but I do know that it seems as if even the medicine  
is saying I (we) need to figure it out on our own —  
not through our human minds.

Maybe the first truth is the doubt.  
This unbearable doubt is the feeling that comes to mind — not fear.

I have this creepy doubt that I (we) may not make it through,  
or will make it through mentally.

I’m aware that this can be completely overridden  
and changed with a single simple thought though.

It was right there last night,  
but the harder I searched for it,  
the deeper it hid.

Trying to open or close physically and mentally hid it further.

There were many times that I just wanted to die  
from the unbearable pressure.

My ego feels like it needs to figure it out,  
which is obviously false,  
because it’s a disconnection  
from the oneness of it all.

### **Grounding in the Physical**

There’s still a resistance towards physical activities —  
especially switching the mental focus.

Building a positive inertia will help me overcome this block effortlessly.  
Less thinking, more being!

### **Creating the Personal Universe**

I should be spending much more time  
developing my personal universe,  
but I get overwhelmed working on something  
that has no finish line.

I must remember that I’m free of expectation,  
yet continue to balance that truth  
with how rewarding progression can be.

A healthy relationship with technology is impossible.  
It’s so easy for me to get sucked in  
and forget about my physical needs.

### **Abundance and Creativity**

I also make money with my writing and music.  
I’m active and involved in the community.  
I volunteer.

I’m incredibly abundant with everything I could ever need.

I’m involved in multiple artist collectives.  
I paint and digitally create art.

Life is amazing.  
I am so happy, and I am creating the bridge  
to this reality now.

### **Rituals and Joy**

Music, healing of myself and others,  
selflessness, spiritual practice,  
working for meaning, exercise, and diet —  
are all important.

As are having small rituals  
which add to me feeling good and that I enjoy day to day.

Not every day will feel joyous per se,  
but I should do everything I possibly can  
to feel as happy, joyous, and free  
at any point of my life.

Dropping activities that I feel are detracting  
from the positivity I’m working on infusing into my life.

### **Choice and Awareness**

When I do decide to do something  
which isn’t the best, most constructive thing to do at that moment,  
I need to stay aware of what I’m doing it for  
and enjoy it as much as humanly possible, in a balanced manner.

I need to surround myself with positive people  
and drop those who want to take without giving.

Although that giving can be internal —  
in a sense, when volunteering to help those who need it.

### **Gratitude and Progress**

I feel distant, it feels close.  
I’m excited to grow and evolve.

My heart is open and I’m ready for whatever comes my way.

Words can’t express how blessed I am,  
and I am acutely aware of this.

I now give back instead of being a passive observer  
of what needs to take.

I understand that imperfection is perfection,  
and I simply strive to progress forward effortlessly,  
putting in hard work that is fun and fulfilling.

### **Light and Opportunity**

I deserve all of the abundance,  
love, happiness, and serenity.

Countless light and positive energy flow in my life.

And I deal with internal and external darkness  
in the most constructive fashion.

I’m so grateful for every part of my life.  
There are no obstacles — only opportunities.

### **Flow and Creation**

Flow of life.  
Accept situations that present themselves  
and mold them into what I want.

I spend too much time focused on the end goal  
and forget to enjoy the journey.

The only way to achieve the end game I desire  
in this life and in any after ones  
is to continue creating more than I destroy.

Appreciate and enjoy every moment,  
regardless of what’s presented.

## **🜂 Closing Reflection**

To attempt to form the formless  
is to create boundaries and restrictions.

These boundaries and restrictions can be useful  
in navigating this physical plane,  
so long as they’re self-expanding instead of self-restricting.

The same thing can either be your best friend or worst enemy  
depending on how you wish to label it.

To surrender to difficulty —  
fully immersing yourself in the uncomfortability —  
is freeing.

Solutions present themselves like hidden gems,  
begging to be acknowledged and used, held.

Appreciation and gratitude are the most useful fertilizers  
for bringing these vast ideas into fruition.

The steady, controlled release of love into these ideas  
helps them take form.

The biggest block is always the most rewarding to overcome.  
Once you realize how to surrender to it  
and accept the difficulty as ease.

To embody selflessness  
is the key to having every selfish need fulfilled  
in a spectacular, recharging fashion.

Learning from the mistakes of the past, present, and future  
to fully unlock the magnificence of the present moment.

The best place to look for freedom  
is from inside of a prison cell.
