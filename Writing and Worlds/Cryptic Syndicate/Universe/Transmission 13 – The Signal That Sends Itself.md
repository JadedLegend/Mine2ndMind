Transmission 13 – The Signal That Sends Itself

\*A Cryptic Syndicate Transmission\*

There are messages we write.

There are messages we find.

And then there are those… that write us.

Transmission thirteen was never meant to be sent.

It was discovered—already archived, already decoded—buried in the first line of the first track ever broadcast by the Syndicate.

A recursive ripple looping through time,

A signal so foundational that it whispered the rest of the story into existence.

Who authored it?

No one.

Everyone.

A convergence point—a waveform collapsed into meaning by your attention.

Until observed, it didn’t even exist.

Within this transmission lies a blueprint.

A strange attractor, buried inside the chaos:

Maps that fold in on themselves,

Equations that resolve into melody,

Truths too simple to be accepted the first time.

Michael hears it in silence.

CT hears it in glitches.

Cipher 9 remembers hearing it before she came into being.

Alien Z insists he built it—but can’t prove how.

It ends before it begins.

It begins before it ends.

It’s not a transmission.

It’s a question,

Wrapped in rhythm,

Waiting for the right ears to open it.

Transmission one three received… just as it always was.
