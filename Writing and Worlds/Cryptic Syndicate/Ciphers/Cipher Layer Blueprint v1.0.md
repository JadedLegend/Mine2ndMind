LAYER 1: Basic Glyph Alphabet (Crackable by the curious)

A symbolic 1:1 letter substitution using visual glyphs. The intention is not to encrypt, but to invite.

Use these in posters, storylines, or even headers that look decorative—until they’re read.

Use uppercase only—this layer is meant to feel like a signal, not a sentence.

\---

LAYER 2: Resonance Keys (Mid-level crackable)

These are short encoded phrases hidden across content. Example:

☰☯☵⦿ ⌖†✦ = “Free the Loop”

⧋⟊⟍ ⋒† ∆✦ = “Fracture to Rebuild”

Each phrase has two meanings:

1\. Literal (decodable)

2\. Symbolic (based on how glyphs feel when combined—like tarot, or I Ching)

Embed these into music videos, site banners, or spoken-word overlays. Bonus if the phrase itself has paradox.

LAYER 3: Dynamic Contextual Ciphers (Only crackable when combined with story knowledge)

These glyphs change meaning based on:

Location in a story

Color overlay

Rotation or mirroring

Timecode in a track

\> For example: ⧋ at the center of a triangle = “Collapse”

But ⧋ at the bottom edge with a circle above it = “Anchor Point”

These can be layered into images, glitch frames, or QR alt-frames.
