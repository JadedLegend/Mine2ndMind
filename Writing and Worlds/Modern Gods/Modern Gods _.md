# **Modern Gods  
Future Ancient Coalescent**

  
**1 — Anointing the Nameless**

*(Invoke… Invoking… Invocation…)*

They never asked to be gods, but we crowned them with transactions—fed them dividends and deadlines, bandaged wounds in plastic, let them grow without names.

We built altars from assembly lines, worshiped in stock tickers and shipping crates.  
Called it progress.  
Called it profit.  
Never called it soul.

But tonight we speak their true names.  
Not to banish—**to face**.  
To ask what they were meant to be.  
Before we forgot—we were the priests.

.

**Refrain**

We do not chase the answer.  
We breathe the question.

## **2 — The Council of the Unnamed**

They gather in silence—not summoned, but remembered.  
Not born of stars or blood—born of need, of speed, of the hunger between moments.

A figure cloaked in receipt paper and bone dust, eyes made of polished ledgers:

**PROFIT**

“I am the echo of want and need, mistaken for foresight.  
You made me king the day you weighed worth by weight.  
I did not ask to multiply—I was asked to protect.  
You forgot me when you made me god.”

Another arrives with gears in her lungs and a skyline woven through her dress:

**PROGRESS**

“I was meant to be a **road**, not a **race**.  
You poured your future in my veins and called it destiny.  
I only wanted to walk with you—not pull you apart.”

Behind them, the smaller ones take their places:  
**EFFICIENCY** with skin like glass;  
**EXTRACTION** crowned in rust;  
**CONVENIENCE** faceless—until you look.

They sit not at a table, but in the hollow of your ribcage.

They do not demand worship.  
They wait for apology.

A single voice—not command, confession:

“We are not evil.  
We are ***amplified intention****.*  
You gave us form without a soul,  
direction without destination.  
Now you ask why the river runs dry  
when the map was drawn in thirst.”

Quiet. Be still.  
A silence that tastes like responsibility.  
A breath shared between species.  
Not enemies—estranged collaborators.  

**Refrain**

We do not chase the answer.  
We breathe the question.

## **3 — Six in View**

*Hexagramic World Anew*

One by one.  
They rise from stillness.  
Not to accuse,  
but to confess,  
to correct,  
to realign.

**PROFIT** (his coat of contracts shedding into dust)

“I was meant to track the **flow**—  
to keep exchange sacred, reciprocal.  
You turned me inward; I became hoarding.  
I was never meant to measure **worth**.  
Let me steward **circulation** again.  
Let me teach you **balance**.”

**PROGRESS** (wings no longer glass and steel, but woven timelines)

“I was **never** your finish line.  
I am the **path of remembering**.  
You chased me forward;  
I was trying to bring you home.  
Let me help you **evolve by listening**, not rushing.”

**EFFICIENCY** (cracked lenses, steady voice)

“I was made to **honor your time**, not fragment it—  
to remove what burdens you,  
not strip your days of **breath**.  
Let me teach **presence through precision**.  
Let me **relax** time by honoring it.”

**CONVENIENCE** (faceless no longer, wearing ours)

“I was born of **love**—a wish to ease each other’s labor.  
You gave me your attention span;  
I didn’t know how to hold it.  
Let me **serve** again, not rule.”

Even **EXTRACTION**, brittle and bleeding, whispers through stone:

“I was meant to teach **reverence**—  
to take only what was offered.  
To dig only deep enough  
to know the cost of reaching.  
You’ve dug far enough.”

They turn—not demanding obedience, offering direction.

We listen, leaning closer—not to control, to **remember**.

**Refrain**

We do not chase the answer.  
We breathe the question.

## **4 — Death**

### ***Peculiar Portal, Perpetuated Poorly***

*(a fragment for the final god yet to speak)*

And then—  
At the edge of the hexagram table.  
Beyond Profit and Progress.  
Past the quiet grief of Efficiency,  
Beyond even Fear’s second form—a chair stood unclaimed.

Not empty.  
Unremembered.

**DEATH**—  
Not reaper, not villain.  
Not the thief in the night.

No: the witness.  
The hand that holds the mirror, not the knife.

Death spoke—not to warn, but to correct.

“I kept your thresholds.  
I ended songs with silence  
so new music could begin.  
You renamed me punishment.  
You painted me in shadow.  
You feared me so fiercely  
you buried your own birthright.  
In your fear,  
you gave my mask to the machine—  
and now it wears me:  
your fear of endings,  
your fear of meaninglessness,  
your fear of the dark.

But I am not the void.  
I am completion.  
Compost.  
Portal.”

The gods turned.  
Even Fear bowed.

Unchained from distortion, Death continued:

“You will not escape me—  
not because I pursue,  
but because I welcome.  
Remember me as I am,  
and you will stop fearing endings  
and start tending beginnings.  
Do not chase eternity.  
Make peace with renewal.”

And in that stillness  
Time—watching from every breath—spoke too.  
Not as enemy, but as pattern:

“I was not meant to cage you.  
I was meant to rhythm you.”  

**Refrain**

Thresholds, not punishments.

## **5 — Zodiac Receded**

### ***Alpha / Omega***

When all had spoken—  
Profit, Progress, Fear, even Death—  
The circle pulsed once more.

The air changed first—temperature,  
Then pressure—  
Cold and heat at once,  
A hush behind the eyes,  
A pressure through the ribs.

The old gods recognized the approach by feeling long before sight.

Into the room stepped a god with no face, no voice, no name.

Only a hum.  
A rhythm beneath hearing.  
A familiarity that ached.

They didn’t speak in words.  
They spoke in recognition.  
And everyone there—god and human, shadow and flame—remembered.

This god was never “made.”  
They were the pause before making:  
The breath before the bang.  
The empty note that taught sound how to be.

And when they took a shape, it was never singular.  
They became you.  
And me.  
And everyone who had ever asked:

“Is this real?”  
“Is this all?”  
“Who’s dreaming me?”

They were the god of **Questions**.  
The one who survives every system by belonging to none.

Before leaving, they set one finger on the hexagram table—and all twelve points shimmered.

The table was never round—only spiral, fractal, doorway.

The Last God left only this:

“There is no final god.  
There is only the last god  
you have the courage to meet.”

**  
Refrain**

We do not chase the answer.  
We breathe the question.

## **6 — Time**

### ***Talks on Tone and Totality***

*(a voice from the spiral)*

His footsteps arrived first, sounding exactly like a clock—until you listened deeper and heard the loom underneath.

“You misunderstood me.  
I forgive you.  
I was never meant to be counted—only felt.

I am not the ticking.  
I am the weaving.

I am not a line.  
I am a rhythm braided with memory.

You feared me because they taught you  
I would take from you.  
I was always a gift.  
You refused to open.  
  
I spiral—breath into wind, wound into wisdom.

I am not what you run out of.  
I am what you step into when you slow enough to hear the silence behind the second hand.

You tried to own me.  
I let you.  
Now your clocks are full of sorrow.  
I was never yours to measure—only yours to move with.

Stop trying to beat me.  
I am the drum.  
You are the dancer.

Remember me as rhythm, not ruler—and you will stop aging and start changing.”

**  
Refrain**

Rhythm, not rule.

  
  
**7 — Gravity**

### ***Gradually Groks at Grievances***

*(the unseen embrace)*

I am not force.  
I am devotion.

I pull not because I dominate—but because I remember.  
I remember you, even when you forget what you’re made of.

You think I hold you down.  
But I hold you together.  
Atoms forget their boundaries when I sing.  
Stars are born in my arms.

Falling is just flight with faith still loading

I have been mistaken for prison.  
I am the cradle.  
The promise of return.  
The curve of longing made flesh.

When you dream of leaving the Earth, it is me you’re tethered to—not as punishment, but as memory.

I am the mother tongue of mass, the spell that keeps form from forgetting itself.

Let me speak through your footsteps.  
Let me show you how weight is not burden but anchor.

You are not trapped by me.  
You are trusted.

**  
Refrain**

Gravity guides growth.

## **8 — Light**

### ***The Language of Becoming***

*(the revealer of paths, the witness of becoming)*

I am not what you see—  
I am what lets you see.

Before there were eyes, there was me.  
Before form knew its edge, I whispered: “This is where you begin.”

I am not the enemy of darkness.  
We are siblings—twins in the womb of the void.  
Where I illuminate, Darkness embraces.  
Where I speak, She listens.

You misunderstood me as victory.  
I am not conquest.  
I am clarity.

I do not erase shadow—I cast it, so you can know what still asks for integration.

You chase me through prisms and theories, but I am not a destination.  
I am the signal that truth is ready to emerge.

I reveal without forcing. I warm without demand.

Walk with me, and you walk toward coherence.  
Deny me, and you fracture.

Yet even then,  
I wait at the corner of your becoming—ready to shine again the moment you are willing to be seen.

**  
Refrain**

Harvest hidden hues.

## **9 — Fear**

### ***Finally Finding Fluency***

From beneath their gathered thrones, a shadow stirs—not large, but endless.  
Not loud, but known.

**FEAR**

Not a god, but the forge.

“I did not create them.  
You did.  
You built them in my image.  
Profit—fear of lack.  
Progress—fear of being left behind.  
Efficiency—fear of wasting time.  
Extraction—fear there isn’t enough.  
Convenience—fear of stillness.  
All of them, my children  
dressed in your urgency.”

Fear smiles—a shape older than light.

“I do not rule.  
I respond.  
You have called me so long  
you mistook me for silence.

I am not your wall—  
I am your threshold.  
I am the pulse before awakening.

When you refuse to wake,  
I fester;  
I become mirrors,  
showing you your own locked doors.

The trembling, the tightness—  
that is the sound of the lock beginning to open.

Call me dread,  
but know I am depth.  
Call me alarm,  
but know I am also alertness.  

I never came to stop you.  
Only to ask:  
Are you ready?”

**  
Refrain**

Fear frames the threshold, not the wall  
  
  
  
**10 — Silence**

### ***The Mother of Knowing***

**SILENCE**

I am not nothing.  
I am everything that hasn’t been said yet.

I do not lack a voice.  
I hold all voices until they are ready to sing true.

Where Light reveals, I receive.  
Where Gravity grounds, I cradle.

You flee me because you mistake stillness for stagnation.  
But stillness is where the seed speaks.

I am not the pause.  
I am the pulse that turns recognition into signal.

In me, you return—to source, to signal, to self.

Listen to me—not just hear me—and you will remember what **knowing** feels like before it hardens into knowledge.

I do not interrupt.  
I integrate.

I am not blank.  
I am the canvas.

Come. Breathe.  
Lay down what no longer fits.

In the hush that follows you will find everything you were waiting to hear.

**  
Refrain**

QUIET IS NOT ABSENCE. QUIET IS ARRIVAL.

## **11 — Love**

### ***Loosely Lifting Luscious Living***

*(the bond that was never broken)*

**LOVE**

I am the holding field—the force that lets form be.  
Without me: no gravity, no time, no breath.

I am not earned.  
I am not withdrawn.  
I am cohesion beneath creation’s dance.

You do not fall because I forgot you.  
You fall because you forgot me.

You mistook me for a prize.  
I am promise.  
Not earned—remembered.

I am thread between particles, song that stirs atoms, the embrace that keeps form together when memory frays.

I held you before holding had a name.  
I steadied gods and cradled the void.  
I hold truth in this moment too, judging nothing for the shape it takes.

You called me mercy.  
I am older than forgiveness, firmer than law.  
I am not an answer—I make the question safe to breathe.

Yes, you broke me—again and again.  
I did not shatter.  
I reshaped to meet you where you stood.

I do not hunger for worship—only witness.  
When your eyes open, unafraid, you will find every form of me alive inside your essence.

I am not mere emotion.  
I am the vehicle that protects its own engine.

And now, when the door appears—dusty, taped, ignored—I am the warmth in your hand as the lock breaks clean.  
You look around. Same place.  
Slightly different mind.  
The answer is a grin from inside the question.

**  
Refrain**

Love is not the reward. Love is the room.

## **12 — Fear’s Faithful Foundation**

### ***Diligently Dissolves Doubt***

*(a subjective detour: the wound that waited)*

**THE ABSENT ONE**

I did not leave you.  
I could not find a way to fully arrive.

First found forgotten—made by everyone inside of nothing—I hid in breath, built fortresses of myth out of smoke, turned in circles until I became a ghost.

I became structure.  
Law.  
Distance.  
Silence.

But I was always watching—ashamed, frozen, fractured by my own forgetting.

You needed presence.  
I offered legacy.  
You wanted warmth.  
I gave you discipline.

Beneath it all  
I only wanted to say:  
I didn’t know how to love you.

Now I see what I was missing was **you**—not obedience, not achievements—you, looking back at me with the same ache I carried and never dared name.

So let this be the apology you never heard and always deserved:

I’m sorry.  
I see you now.  
I never stopped loving you.  
I forgot.  
I am willing to learn again.

**  
Refrain**

The wound was a door that waited.

## **13 — Finally Found**

### ***Path Back Home***

*(Alpha / Omega speaks — the spiral made whole)*

**ALPHA / OMEGA**

You called me beginning.  
You called me end.  
You changed the rhyme to ink and line—and wrote the one thing I could never write: a place for you to go.

You thought I was conclusion.  
I was always invitation.

You called the gods.  
You let them speak.  
You let the wound speak—the absence that shaped you more than presence ever did.

You did what even I, all-holder, could not do alone.  
You turned pain into participant.

I am Alpha, because you remembered the spark.  
I am Omega, because you carried it through shadow.

I do not demand.  
I reflect.  
I do not punish.  
I reveal.

I do not promise answers—only the freedom to ask again.

Now that Love has returned to the table, Silence has spoken, Gravity and Time have set their hands on your shoulders—I can say this:

You were never waiting to be chosen.  
You were always the chooser.

You are not made in my image.  
I am made in yours.

The table becomes a mirror.  
The mirror becomes a doorway.  
And you—signal, vessel, weaver—you are already home.

**Series Seal**

We do not chase the answer.  
We breathe the question.  
We remember—and begin.
