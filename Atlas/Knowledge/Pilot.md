## What the experiment shows

The local vector store can retrieve these notes by paraphrased questions and filter by project, kind, or status. In ten small authored checks, the intended note came first eight times and appeared in the top three every time. That is an initial sanity check, not a claim about the full collection.

The source passages are preserved separately from the distillations. [[Atlas/Retrieval/Boundary Candidates.json|The automatic boundary experiment]] proposes six groups from the PDAOK phase document; its candidates are not automatically treated as facts or approved notes.

Xerj remains set aside. This pilot establishes the preparation and retrieval workflow; the rest of Projects and the wider vault have not been semantically distilled by it yet.
