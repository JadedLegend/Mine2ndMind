# Flowtion gives its four spaces different roles

The proposed Flowtion spaces are Focus for making, Dream for exploring, Reflect for making sense or deciding, and Synthesize for compressing, publishing, or handing off. Each space remembers locally while remaining able to sense the others; the source calls this porous memory.
