## Paradox Trader — Inquiry can stay open

An inquiry has its own durable record and can remain exploratory, be paused, or be revisited. A proposal, review, mock execution, and later interpretation each have a separate record. The documented pilot limits execution to a fictional contract and preserves its lineage for review.

## Paradox Trader — Keep originals traceable

The shared library preserves source documents and indexes bounded passages with source names and locators. Agents can choose whether to browse. The active working orientation is stored separately and stays small enough for routine use.

Sources — [[Projects/Paradox Trader/Shared original-document library|Shared original-document library]] · [[Projects/Paradox Trader/Community orientation and quiet rest|Community orientation and quiet rest]]

## Record a new decision or solution

Give it a searchable heading with the project and the question it answers. In a few lines, capture the choice, why it fit, and when it applies. Link the note, chat, or original document that supports it. If a choice changes later, add a dated update and link the earlier record.
