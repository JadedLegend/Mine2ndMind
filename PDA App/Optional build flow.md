### **🜂 Visual Build Flow (the resonance map)**

each “time” module (Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Late) behaves like a breath segment.

#### **inhale — *invitation + orientation***

- the title (e.g., *Integration & Release*) and time read act as your *invocation line*.
- you can add a faint **breathing glow** behind the time — very slow fade in/out (~7s cycle).

#### **hold — *anchors expand***

- the expandable anchor cards (Lighting shift, Water ritual, etc.) are perfect as “resonance nodes.”
- for deeper coherence, try a **translucent overlay** when an anchor is expanded — like a bubble of attention.
- tone: ivory or sand in daylight, violet or indigo at night.

#### **exhale — *craft + reflect***

- bottom buttons are your exhale: the action to externalize what was felt.
- their positioning and softness already create safety. keep the rounded edges; maybe add **slight pulsation** when the user hasn’t engaged for a while (like the app is softly breathing, waiting).
