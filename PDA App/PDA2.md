🩶Style Block Background Redesign Intent

Current issue:  
Each style block background. The background behind each and every entry on the day pages. It feels like a flat stripe that interrupts the atmospheric flow. It’s too dense, too rectangular, and doesn’t harmonize with the translucent panels below it.

Desired feel:

The style blocks should fade into the background, not sit on top of it.

Think of it more as a glow field or soft header layer — a space for orientation, not separation.

It should carry the tone of the time of day (morning gold, afternoon aqua, evening rose, late-night indigo) but at very low opacity, like light in fog.

The text (“Descent and safety”) should appear to float, with enough contrast to read but no heavy shadow or hard edge.

Visual language description:

Background color: same hue family as page background, but 5–10% darker or lighter.

Transparency: around 60–75% — so you can still feel the background through it.

Blur: subtle (4–8px) to separate text from background without forming a block.

Shape: gently rounded edges or a seamless fade at the bottom (no hard rectangle).

Optional: add a faint inner glow or gradient that moves vertically (lighter at top, darker at bottom).

Mood cues by time:

Morning → a pale honey mist.

Afternoon → soft glass with aqua reflection.

Evening → faint rose gradient with golden text.

Late Night → indigo haze, text in soft silver-gray.
