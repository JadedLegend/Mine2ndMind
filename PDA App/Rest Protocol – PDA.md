Rest Protocol – PDA.OK

State: Integration / Recovery / Listening

Signal: Diminished drive, diffuse attention, quiet curiosity.

\---

1\. Recognition

\> “This is not absence. This is digestion.”

Mark the shift from output to absorption. Your system is processing what it built; nothing is lost.

\---

2\. Core Practices

Presence over productivity. Do one thing at a time and let it finish itself.

Gentle motion. Walk, stretch, tinker, breathe; movement without agenda.

Low-stimulus input. Music, light reading, nature, texture—feed the senses, not the algorithms.

Micro-expression. A short note, photo, or message that lets the creative current exhale.

\---

3\. Environmental cues

Element Nourishes Drains

Natural light, warm air parasympathetic grounding harsh light, loud HVAC

Soft audio loops rhythmic coherence fragmented media

Simple foods, hydration gentle metabolic rhythm stimulants, excess sugar

\---

4\. Reflection Prompt

\> “What wants to rest through me?”

Instead of asking what you should do, listen for what your system is already doing quietly. Record one sentence in PDA.OK if it speaks.

\---

5\. Re-entry Marker

When curiosity starts to condense into focus—when you want to open your editor, or reorganize a file—note the date. That’s the field turning toward action again. Resume from there without apology.
