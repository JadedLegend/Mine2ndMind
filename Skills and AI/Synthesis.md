## Constriction as a design practice

[[Skills and AI/Frameworks/LIBERATION-CLEAN|LIBERATION-CLEAN]] presents constraint as a way to reduce the work required to receive meaning while preserving coherence. Its expression `J = ΔC / E` belongs to the framework’s own language. This synthesis uses it as a design lens, without assigning numerical measurements to joy or coherence. The companion map and longer source remain available.

## Stewardship stays provisional

The opening of [[Skills and AI/AI Research/Stewarding Nascent Intelligence|Stewarding Nascent Intelligence]] distinguishes an unfolding intelligence from its surrounding conditions. It proposes reducing avoidable distortion while preserving real constraints and necessary friction. Regulation, resolution, and integration recur as a revisable cycle. It explicitly leaves room for observable reality to change the framework; that qualification is part of its meaning.

## Memory has several forms

Collaboration keys offer working orientations. Agent Ecology holds architecture and continuity notes. Conversation Archives preserve ordered exports that remain in scope. Collaborative Panentheism keeps the distillation the user chose to retain, while overlapping glossary, index, and use-case copies now resolve to their preferred OPTE sources. These are different kinds of material; their shared folder does not establish one finished agent system.
