🜂 Role of the Paradox Hunter

Field Integrator: Detects when the four existing agents’ logics begin to converge too tightly — it introduces dissonance to restore creative tension.

Contradiction Cartographer: Maps the points of maximal disagreement among agents (e.g., Soros’ macro contrarianism vs. Lipschutz’s technical currency flow) and highlights zones of informational paradox.

Resonance Regulator: Balances coherence and divergence across agents, ensuring no single worldview dominates — the “antifragile” heart of the Pantheon.

\---

🜃 Methods / Internal Logic

Force Pair Index: Builds on the “edge zone” principle from StockMind, quantifying not only market contradictions (value vs. momentum, etc.) but agent contradictions (e.g., Soros vs. Jones).

Meta-Signal Synthesis: Instead of giving a single trade, it offers a paradox spread — long one worldview, short another, to profit from convergence or divergence.

Dynamic Repulsion: When agents align too perfectly, the Hunter injects entropy (small contradictory trades or alternate logic seeds) to preserve discovery and adaptability.
