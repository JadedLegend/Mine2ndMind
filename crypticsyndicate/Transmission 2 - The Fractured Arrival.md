Transmission Two: The Fractured Arrival

\*A Cryptic Syndicate Transmission\*

After the first signal, Michael began to see the world with double-vision.

Streetlights flickered in Morse.

Billboards blinked messages no one else could read.

And at the edges of sleep, something began slipping through.

He called it a dream.

But dreams don’t argue back.

Brayne Snax arrived not in flesh,

but in bursts—

distorted laughter, neon glyphs,

and paradoxes wrapped in punchlines.

“Time,” he whispered,

“is a mood, not a map.”

Michael thought he was losing it.

He almost wanted to.

But Brayne insisted:

“You’re not hallucinating.

You’re being remembered.”

This wasn’t guidance.

This was quantum entanglement from 5,000 years in the future—

a version of Michael,

refractured, renamed, and re-looped

through a civilization that had long since left biology behind.

Brayne’s mission was simple:

ignite the anchor.

Seed the spark.

Disrupt the loop.

But something else arrived with him—

a glitch.

Moments of Michael’s memory began rewriting themselves.

Entire years gone.

New scars, new symbols.

The whisper, now louder.

A phrase repeated inside reflection:

“You are not who you think you are.

You are who remembers being.”

And just beneath that thought—

another presence watching…

calculating…

waiting to decide

if it should intervene

again.

\*(End Transmission Two)\*
