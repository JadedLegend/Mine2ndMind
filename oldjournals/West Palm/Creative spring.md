The creative spring which resides in my center has been blocked off for some time. It’s beginning to trickle through and this paper is an attempt to break the dams which have been placed.

I just put the pen to paper free of judgment, allowing my consciousness to be as free as it can comfortably be.

I am incredibly grateful to be getting back in touch with this part of my being — the well which has been in my heart and soul, and seems to fill with love and joy whenever I quit resisting myself.

The more I put myself in situations of difficulty, which I have made terrifying in my head, the more I realize the real difficulty lies in not doing these things. Whether they’re physical, social, spiritual, or emotional, the resistance seems to fade when I concentrate on the presence which is beyond me and can get out from behind my egotistical self-absorption.
