It’s Love

Every once in a while I look at the world around me and I am struck with awe by the beauty of it all. Everything is so vibrant and alive, even inanimate objects. The colors pop, the waves ripple. A million shades of blue and green cast at you, a skyline which meets the magnificent sky with only a few clouds on the horizon.

The most brilliant hues of blue are suddenly all around me. The cars drive over the bridge like worker ants serving the Queen. The naturally unnatural interwoven interconnectedness of all is awe inspiring beyond words.

A feeling that has no filled reality that can encapsulate the incompleteness it is. All that can encapsulate is love. Two sides of the same coin. Even the agony is love.

Some set out searching for what it already possesses. Searching externally for a validation which has laid in hibernation internally for eons. Waiting to be recognized. Patiently and delicately pulling towards the love.
