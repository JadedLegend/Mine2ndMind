Maybe my ego has made yet another mask.

In order to stop the quest for knowledge,

it's telling me there are no absolute truths—

so why even think about it,

or try to process anything?

This mask may be more healthy

than the mask that wants all the answers.

I feel, perhaps, like there are no answers in the silence.

This may be untrue—just like so many other paradoxes.

The silence may be much more than what I take it for.

It is what I can closest describe as feeling,

but lack of what I'm used to describing as such.

An infinite source of pure intuition, unadulterated being.

I get lost in the silence.

Unable to feel the gradual pull of my soul.

Searching for distraction.

Unwilling to listen.

Trying to listen too hard.

Not reality.

What I'm listening for is not words, or feeling, or anything I can put words too.

I'm "listening" for the presence of being.

My current goal is to be present

and allow this reality to unfold.

How do I decide which intention to place?

My immediate intention is

to stay balanced, grounded, and aligned.

To stay in recovery.

To be healthy.

To exercise.

To get a job.

To get a car.

To get an apartment.

To reengage spiritual disciplines to help this.

To place myself first as my ultimate priority,

create healthy boundaries,

to center within.

I wish to develop and grow as a person.

In order to do this,

I must dive headfirst into uncomfortability and fear.

This is challenging,

because my lower self takes this as a threat.

Once I immerse myself in these discomforts,

I always realize there is nothing to fear.

Fear is there for us to survive.

I want to live.
