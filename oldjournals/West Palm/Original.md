Gpt remix

A million answers to a billion questions,

spinning over and over —

truth hidden in a puzzle

too vast for the human mind to hold.

At last I see:

it’s time to live inside the question

instead of tearing myself apart

searching for answers.

So simple.

How did I miss it?

The paradox cuts both ways:

I resisted with surrender,

not realizing that surrender itself

was the only key.

I feared the answers,

yet they dissolved the moment I stopped chasing.

A maze shifting its shape as I moved,

rewriting the story again and again,

until simplicity blazed so bright

I could only squint.

Now I dive with open eyes

into the depths of pain

to find an everlasting comfort,

contradictory as sunlight

that burns the skin to blisters

yet heals as it scars.

Nature cycles — that is its nature.

The apprentice becomes the master

in the breathless instant

between life and death.

Cold as ice,

and yet —

oh, so bright, so warm.
