## Lyrics as a place to think and sound

The source collection includes individual songs, revisions, creative corpora, artist-identity writing, and a numbered MurMwurM body. This synthesis draws on selected lyrics and the EP concept; the full index preserves access to every retained music note.

## MurMwurM: two presences in relation

The [[Music/MurMwurM/5 song EP|five-track EP concept]] names MURM as Melodic Unification Remembrance Model and WURM as Wondrous Underground Resonance Manifestation. Its proposed movement runs through invocation, awakening, crossing, unearthed memory, and integration. The production language ranges from ambient and layered voices to subterranean bass, glitch, and rhythmic tension. These are documented creative directions, not a report of completed recordings.

[[Music/MurMwurM/1 MurMwurM|The opening MurMwurM lyric]] and [[Music/MurMwurM/2 The Silence That Sang First|The Silence That Sang First]] return to breath, silence, remembering, origins, and the passage between apparent opposites. Their numbered texts remain available as writing in their own rhythm. The concept’s five tracks and the retained numbered lyric files are separate source structures; this migration does not force a one-to-one track list between them.

## Older songs carry a broader archive

The sampled [[Music/Songs and Lyrics/(New) Harmony in Chaos|Harmony in Chaos]] searches for steadiness amid mental and social pressure. [[Music/Songs and Lyrics/Decoding the Universe|Decoding the Universe]] uses chorus, verses, bridge, and outro to move through cosmic imagery, uncertainty, and transformation. These examples suggest recurring connections with the wider collection, while leaving the rest of the songs to speak individually.
