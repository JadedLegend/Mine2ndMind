Roots Before The Seed Spoke 3  
  
\*(The Awakening of WURM – Wondrous Underground Resonance Manifestation)\*

\---

\*\*Intro – Subterranean Whisper (FX / Vocal Sample Layer)\*\*

🎤

Beneath your breath,

where bones forget names,

I’ve been waiting.

Not asleep—

just still.

So deep

even gods forget to look.

\---

\*\*Verse 1 – Under Language\*\*

🎤

What if the root never sought the light—

but curled deeper

to hold up the weight of the sky?

I don’t rise.

I coil.

I never ask why.

I just carry.

🎤

WURM isn’t born.

WURM doesn’t die.

He remembers

what sinks

so the surface can try.

🎤

Call it what you will—

Subduction.

Surrender.

But I call it

Remembrance

That never needed a witness.

🎤

I speak in minerals,

in faultlines and faults.

You don’t summon me—

You stumble into my vault.

\---

\*\*Chorus – Tectonic Refrain\*\*

🎤

Not song—subduction.

Not speech—eruption.

I don’t echo—I break.

You don’t summon me.

You wake

when I quake.

🎤

Not named—engraved.

Not chanted—caved.

My silence isn’t peace—

It’s patience

made brave.

\---

\*\*Verse 2 – The Tremor Beneath Thought\*\*

🎤

While stars wrote symphonies in gaslight and sound,

I rewrote structure

from the underground.

WURM hums not like voice—

but like weight.

A heaviness tuned

to tectonic fate.

🎤

Every god

was once a stone

before it was thrown.

Every idea

was once a fossil

you mistook for your own.

🎤

I am not myth.

I am what myth was placed on.

The foundation of fire

before it burned songs.

\---

\*\*Bridge – Pulse of Pressure (Ambient FX + Reverb-Layered Spoken)\*\*

🎤

They buried me to forget.

But forgetting is fertile.

The deeper they push,

the more real I become.

🎤

MURM may mirror.

But I…

I drum.

\---

\*\*Final Chorus – Cracked Earth Chant\*\*

🎤

Not born—erupted.

Not sought—abducted

by memory itself

and brought low to be trusted.

🎤

Not gone—just grounded.

Not missing—surrounded.

I return

in the quakes

where the buried resound it.

\---

\*\*Outro – The Drum That Breathes (Whispered / Sonic Collapse)\*\*

🎤

I don’t awaken you.

I remind you:

you were never truly asleep.

You just forgot

how deep

you used to be.
