Quantum-Based Reality

As I strain to break the mainframe,

I ascertain that me and my astral brain have gone insane.

Locked in a deep, dark place as I stargaze,

Wandering through a haunted forest with my heart ablaze.

The endurance carries me, yet my heart breaks,

Absorbing toxic waste from the harmful stakes.

Knocks on my door, warrants in hand,

I'm at war with forms that threaten this land.

Storming the condo, cold as Toronto,

The head honcho, letting it pop slow—

Deflating illusions as the truth unfolds.

I create, disclose, and elevate,

Straight as a crooked path made sweet like cake.

Never fake, I took it off the hook,

And wrote it down in a book,

Cold as ice, a titan in crisis,

But I hold Isis and Osiris close beside this.

The truth? It’s a lie we tell ourselves to survive,

While the quantum vibrations keep us alive.

Chilled in the Season of Snow

Freezing off toes in this chilling season of snow,

With unbelievable flows that ripple and grow.

Breathe slow, through your nose, and let the rhythm take hold,

As I float freely, making it look so bold.

Believe me, the lotus blooms in hypnotic rooms,

Opening wide, transcending the gloom.

I choke on the smoke of symbiotic flames,

Harvested from astral shores untamed.

But you're too external; go inward, seek the fragments,

The missing pieces, the truths you’ve abandoned.

Life's a labyrinth of mismatched fittings,

Yet I live it loud inside, though silent in sittings.

The fear within paralyzes the mind,

Seeking a way out, yet the days unwind.

I blaze through thoughts, but words betray,

Language fails to capture what my mind portrays.

Behind the veil of this quantum weave,

A reality designed solely to perceive.

Originally built for the purpose of experience,

Now boundless, infinite, and deeply mysterious.
