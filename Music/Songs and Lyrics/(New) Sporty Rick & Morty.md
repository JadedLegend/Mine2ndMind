Floating in from the outskirts of my inner sanctum,

I limit my design and existence, a prisoner hidden within a system.

Encoded in imprinted DNA, I wrote it down, cursive spirals submerged in waves,

Emotions unloaded, hoping to lose control quick of these tidal displacements.

Faced with an ancient mission, yet plugged into separation and division,

I patiently listen to silence, trusting divinity with repetition.

But it seems to let me down repeatedly—mental energy oscillates,

Frowning one moment, smiling the next, an unstable state.

I'm searching for the protocol, a remedy to clear this energy,

Effigies burned in a ritual of learning, wisdom yearning endlessly.

The sherm burns slow through a wormhole, I step beyond mortality,

Clearing portals and marveling at visions of clarity.

Often I’m the boiling pot calling the kettle black,

Counteracting my ignorance with lyrical attacks.

Indecisive wisdom darts like shadows; advice becomes inadvisable,

Yet I strive to find a tune to move to, a rhythm undeniable.

Blew through dimensions, hit 3:40, became Morty, you be Rick,

This lyrical discussion drifts into a cosmic split.

Each word a ripple, each rhyme a key, unlocking layers of infinity.

This is more than poetry—it’s alchemy, fusing the lost fragments of me.
