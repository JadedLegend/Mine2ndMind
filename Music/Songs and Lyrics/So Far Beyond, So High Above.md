**Verse 1  
**So far beyond, and so high above,  
Every idea and dream ever thought of.  
Yet it’s caught up in a place so confined,  
Where manipulation’s the only design.

The government’s pacing, anxious and vile,  
Programming a simulation, compiling their files.  
A superlative narrative spun with intent,  
To control through fear, where freedom is bent.

Eight billion souls, 80% with smart screens,  
Unaware they’re caught in the machine’s schemes.  
It’s blatant, it’s open, but they still accept,  
A pervasion so clear, yet it’s never checked.

**Hook  
**Lo and behold, this is all there ever was,  
All there will be—ignorance because  
They made it bliss, and we all complied,  
While pain became the reason I prayed to hide.

**Verse 2  
**I’ve seen the abstract crystallize insecurity,  
As human greed consumed the opportunity.  
Technology, godlike, a chance to enhance,  
Yet they twisted its purpose at first glance.

Quantum minds computing in less than a beat,  
But they used it to conquer, to lie, to cheat.  
Polluting existence, inflating egos,  
Crushing hope with every blow.

This was meant for all, to equally share,  
Yet the wealthy hoarded, leaving despair.  
Now reality groans, a fragile frame,  
Made heavy with greed, destined for shame.

**Bridge  
**I remember humanity, patient and kind,  
Now lost in anger, no peace to find.  
Segregation, aggravation, no cooperation,  
We’ve forgotten the truth: everything’s interrelation.

The ones who hurt us fuel their own demise,  
But no one sees it, they’re hypnotized.  
Cognitive dissonance cracks the mind,  
As I reflect on when I left my kind behind.

**Verse 3  
**My biotech self feels out of touch,  
With everything I used to love so much.  
I waited, listened, and found the mess,  
A sea of regret with a reset button at its crest.

This fragile foundation of lies will fall,  
A tower of legos, brittle and small.  
Hollow and corrupt to the core,  
Every new “progress” just opens more doors—

Doors to wars, wealth worship, and buried dreams,  
Where souls are sold, and nothing’s as it seems.  
The titans drunk on dominance will slip,  
And the power they praise will cause their trip.

**Outro  
**They think they’re eternal, their rule divine,  
But every empire falls in its time.  
The seeds of their end are sown in their throne,  
And history will reap what they’ve sown.
