Creation and Destruction

Creation and destruction,

Two sides, same function, a paradox in conjunction.

Chosen by the touch—mind cracked wide open,

Now I’m smoking fire, my activation’s awoken.

Cosmic forces already wove this thread,

Golden soul but normally not outspoken, instead

I tread lightly through dimensions, transcendence in my mentions.

Lessons deep inside infinity,

Harnessing divine energy through connectivity.

Balancing light and dark, perfect symmetry,

A paradox where I defy conformity.

This is my destiny—what I was born to be.

For so long, I’ve been aware of this clarity.

Given missions and messages, most hit true—

Blessed beyond belief, my G, go ahead, test me, see.

Responsibility’s a tricky thing, a heavy ring,

Means there’s a way to shape this reality,

To reclaim the ability we were born to see.

Surrounding me, I sense the energy.

But without humility, it’s all vanity,

So I relax, break free fake nobility,

Wielding metaphysical artillery.

Oh shit, quantum burn,

After passing the point of no return.

Will I ever learn? Maybe, but I think I’ve earned it—

Why? Because I feel like a whole new person.

Believed to be the son of a sun,

Now it’s time to reclaim my throne as number one.

From creation to destruction, I’ve spun this tale,

A force unbroken, destined to prevail.
