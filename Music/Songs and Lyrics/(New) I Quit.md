I quit it, then I picked it back up—what the fuck?

What’s running through my mind? It happens all the time.

I try to listen, but I get stuck,

Feels like this is my mission—but what the fuck?

Is it really, though? The truth feels strange,

Twisting frames, rearranging my mind state.

To remember it all might break the fall,

But remembrance is a decadence reserved for the few.

Who are you? I am I,

Trying to find the storm’s eye,

The hurricane’s heart, shifting my mind state,

Moving it as far as I can, but it seems stuck—what the fuck?

So I truck to Alaska, chasing something—

This feels like garbage, but I keep running,

Words break down as I’m lost in translation,

A glitch in my mind—pure frustration.
