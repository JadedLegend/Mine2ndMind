Poetry in Motion

Poetry in motion, uncontrollable, incorrigible,

Uncoordinated, insubordinated, unstoppable.

Patience and virtue swirl in a vicious tide,

A sea of lost choices where faith and death collide.

Circling the hordes of mindless drones,

Light creeps out from beneath natural growth.

Unbeknownst to most, the way out is through,

And every exit births another entrance anew.

Mazes, hallways, labyrinthine passages,

All converging toward the same destination.

Oblivious, we pass the sweet antidote waiting,

Drenched in guilt, shame, and tragedies weighing.

Yet the treasure inside, buried beneath the noise,

Lives, forgotten by existence's deafening voice.

Noise and Static

Noise and static, paranoid and emphatic,

Cracked tragic black magic—a ritual erratic.

Searching for certainty in a chasm of doubt,

Something—nothing—the void shouts.

From this nothingness, existence springs,

Purpose breathes in creation’s wings.

To humbly offer oneself to the unknown,

Is to embody purpose, effortlessly sown.

Cacophony of Cognizance

A cacophony of cognizance cascades with force,

From the waterfall’s mouth, it charts its course.

Veins of aquatic architecture carve their path,

Leaving timeworn evidence of eons' wrath.

Smooth over sharp, weight presses the soul,

An endless cycle—creation's control.

Byzantine Confusion

Bamboozled by Byzantine riddles,

Worship relocates, reshapes, belittles.

Peace lies waiting in forbidden fruit,

A knowledge you must taste to reap its root.

Dishonesty, perhaps, is honesty skewed,

An honest lens in dishonest hues.

Florida Man

Oh, Florida Man, why must you thrive in chaos?

Evil, good, or neutral—a melting pot of loss.

Shamelessly embracing sinful emotions,

An open invitation to pain’s commotion.

A friendship forged in strife’s crucible,

A force wild, raw, and inscrutable.

\*\*Eternal Darkness\*\*

The darkness of eternity cascades,

A symphony of colors in shadowed parades.

Heartless uncertainty wears mastery’s guise,

While paradoxical anguish clouds the skies.

It starts where it ends, transcending all speech,

Simple symmetric perfection, forever out of reach.
