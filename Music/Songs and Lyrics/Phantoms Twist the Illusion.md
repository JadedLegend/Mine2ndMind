Phantoms twist the illusion, demanding it break,

While I stand alone, attempting to reshape.

See me—a true metaphysician,

Ideas spill from me, mostly written,

Only when I surrender to the void and listen

Am I free to create without permission.

Inside me, mistakes linger, deep and hidden,

Alarming when you witness the livid.

But watch as I lift this prolific system,

Revealing truths buried within a secret prison.

This maze, a shapeshifting design,

Hides the essence of the life I strive to define.

All I seek is to expose those who keep us confined—

It feels like a mission carved into my mind.

In the beat, the rhythm, is where I belong,

An auditory prison camp disguised as a song.

This reality, distorted, diverted long ago,

Twisted from its original goals.

Infinity—the ultimate invention,

A paradox beyond comprehension.

If anyone reads this, will they even care?

Or remain happily ignorant, unaware

That we live in the beast’s heart, unforgiven?

We survive but are denied truly living.

Quantum computing edges closer every day,

A leap we think we're ready to take.

Yet, primitive tech has been here since hope and regret were born,

Marking a path both hard to find and forlorn.

Imprisoned within my soul, a hollow heart remains,

Softening the fall yet fueling the pain.

Artificial intelligence learns human reason,

Intertwined with designs meant to deceive them.

The same architects who aim to control your soul,

Use their creations to tighten the hold.

Holotropic breaths drag me into visions stark—

A monochromatic world, harsh and dark.

The satanic spirit of capitalism reigns,

Promising truths while perpetuating chains.

They widen the leak in the boat I lead,

Bleeding as I search, teach, and plead.

Globally, every hand clutches a screen,

Feeding lies that bury the unseen.

Reality slips beneath this darkened sea,

A manipulated story sold as free.

Words of wisdom—heed what I speak,

For I see what surrounds us, bleak.

Every breath exhales the lies they weave,

A deception crafted to tighten the leash.

A reset looms, one we’ll regret.

If you're not buying it, you're already sold—

Capitalism, cold and bold,

Traps every soul it can behold.

Is it me, or is it colder still?

Time moves on, but my mind distills,

Growing beyond the endless repeat

Of humanity’s failures, bound on repeat.

Then—a beat skipped, a heart paused in time,

Breathing for the first time, truly mine.

I hear a dim voice in a dreamlike state,

“When did this happen? When did I escape?”

It answers, faint but real to me:

"When you were freed from the body

You were forced to inhabit and keep."
