Permittivity of Free Space

My permittivity gives me efficiency,

A way to channel electricity running through me.

Ordinarily, I wouldn’t speak of my legacy,

But honestly, I think I’m in need of some therapy.

Let’s see the ultimate probability of my abilities—

Probably me, spitting unwritten calligraphy.

Hidden deep within, I’m tripping and dripping,

So listen, please, to these mysterious histories, possibly

Whispers from the future, eventually.

I know there’s gotta be a whole lot of density—

Just breathe, stay in your body, let it be.

Already know I was meant to be

A mentally unstable entity.

Each syllable I spit is oppositional,

I feel invincible when I let it all go.

But it’s hard to overcome the pleasure and pain,

So here’s a measured dose of novocaine.

As I entertain this major brain overdrive,

My dopamine receptors under pressure, so deprived.

Yet somehow, still alive,

I think I may have arrived.

Finally found a way to survive in this

Fucked-up society that’s quietly broken me.

I’m sorry, honestly, but here’s my best apology,

And possibly an agreeable solution—

To free up some room, disperse mental pollution.

I eat mushrooms, take iboga, and listen to music.

It’s therapeutic, the way I use it.

The frequencies align, helping me find

A way to reconnect this fragmented mind.

Electric flows through my permittivity,

Unlocking fragments of hidden divinity.

Here’s my trinity: sound, plant, and symmetry,

Channeling what was meant to be,

A soul finally finding serenity.
