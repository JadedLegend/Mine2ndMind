As I trace the outline of existence,

I’m searching for a way to break my mind’s resistance.

I take the pine, break it up, and twist it,

This relentless approach feels like a medical prescription.

Floating in neurosis, I need to focus,

Closing in, slowing this psychosis,

Caused by a mass hypnosis—

Used to feel so hopeless till I broke the ball and chain.

Now I fall away, decaying at the system’s center,

But I’ll live to fight another day.

I try to escape this broken landscape,

But every plan I make crumbles, and God laughs.

That’s a hard pass—I’m far past

The outer reaches, finding belief in the unseen,

At the throne of something bigger than what’s between.

A scientific approach, I smoke lots and drop hot shit,

Never miss—okay, maybe 75% of the shots I take.

But that 25% that lands is fire,

And I spit it often, fueled by desire.

My rhymes have meaning, though sometimes fleeting,

Caught between the veil and another place—

I hear whispers of things unseen,

Voices rising from the spaces in between.
