Delivering me directly to the heart of the unspoken.

I’m underpaid but overplayed, happy with a listen or a stray.

Today’s mission: insert cognition into your nervous system,

The verses serve to disperse a service of certain wisdom.

Forget capitalism—it’s a prison,

I’m flying underwater on Space DMT, envision.

Catch this net of thoughts; let me breathe my release,

But my psychokinesis crafts ideas, transcending the prison.
