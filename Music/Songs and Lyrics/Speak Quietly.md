Although I know I often speak quietly,  
It’s strange how I’m still viewed as invisible entirely.  
Every day feels like everyone’s rehearsing,  
Playing their parts while I’m left observing, immersed in—  
A system that’s far from aligned,  
Where the muse seems blind, and culture feels confined.

If you listened—truly listened—you’d see the key,  
And through my eyes, you’d catch the irony.  
There’s no fear here; let me make that clear.  
This is more than critique; it’s something sincere.

The truths I drop might feel like bomb threats,  
But honesty is the only currency I’ve kept.  
If you want my soul, there’s no price you can hold.  
I’ve given all I have; control can’t be sold.

Over there, it’s dark—step here into the light,  
Where fear dissolves, and shadows take flight.  
I speak now, diving deep, revealing cracks,  
In a society that’s sprinting toward collapse.

I’ve been smoking the same blunt for what feels like a space month.  
It might not make sense, but that’s the point—  
An opiate wrapped in hidden knowledge,  
Too obscure to be taught in any college.

Sometimes I wonder—  
What’s left of humanity?  
The whole species seems  
Lost in a spiral of vanity.

Life, I believe, was meant to be played for free,  
Not bought, sold, or trapped in this endless debris.  
A maze shifting to keep us blind,  
Distracted by those who control the design.

Faceless ones hoarding dust,  
Passing wealth while we crumble and rust.  
No paywalls—knowledge should be free.  
Instead, we’re stuck in systems failing visibly.

Autonomy, the answer hiding in plain sight,  
Yet we cling to control, afraid to ignite.  
Imagine work so seamless you forget time—  
That’s the freedom, the art, the true design.

But then, I remind myself: “It doesn’t matter.”  
I toss aside my work as dreams shatter.  
Creation becomes just another distraction,  
Lost in a sea of meaningless action.

Still, I see myself as an artisan—  
Chasing mastery, shaping my plans.  
Old work feels like dirt in my hands,  
But with time, I’ve learned its worth expands.

Years spent refining, chasing perfection,  
Only to find it’s a myth, not direction.  
Each mistake, a lesson to build and shape,  
Turning failure into something great
