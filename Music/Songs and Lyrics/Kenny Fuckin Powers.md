Strange how the brain shifts and aligns,

Lit up alive, like stars that shine.

I’m out here, trying to find a couple hits,

Lucy on my lips, let it cleanse me quick.

Unruly grooves cleanse my senses,

Dispensing enlightenment, breaking fences.

Lightning strikes as I’m writing these glyphs,

Etching existence, rewinding resistance.

Loony yet divine, unruly by design,

Deep in my mind, a ruby still shines.

The beauty blinds, I analyze the disguise,

Of archetypes hidden in life’s twisted lines.

Hardly ever right, but I’m still largely nice,

When I sharply slice, colder than ice.

Controller device locked in my mind,

Time’s over, I rhyme slower, flowers I find.

Devouring hours, smoking sour broccoli,

Mandatory stories spill from my laboratory.

My soul takes control, pulling puppet strings,

But I’m on parole, can’t fuck with things.

This joint? From a galaxy far away,

Proud to be an oddity, living free in my way.

Though honestly, too much toxicity,

Still I honor stupidity’s epitome—that’s me.

Friends with the henchmen, blocking ascension,

Lessons etched in my brain’s dimension.

Drastic tactics, magical attachments,

Cracked lips spit tracks that are massively sick.

Addictive facts drip, just sit back and adapt,

To the lyrical habitat, I had to move past

Primordial goo—the truth is a lie.

To live is to die, I’m just here to survive.
