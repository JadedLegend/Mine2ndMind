Looked back, wishing I’d tested it.  
Mysterious powders in countless flavors,  
Dosages modeled on old behaviors.

Nature crafts, but we manipulate,  
Working products, twisting fate.  
Dodging, ducking under all and none,  
Waiting for the moment when the war is won.

Darwinian instincts, my angle intact,  
Revolution whispers in the background’s black.  
Truth, evolution—a patient act,  
Power divided, the system cracked.

**Verse 2  
**Spent a decade chasing the lunatic light,  
No direction, just surviving the fight.  
Rabbit holes deep, tinfoil hats tight,  
Aliens guiding—believed it was right.

Books by Swann and Penre shifted my view,  
Each chapter pulled me closer to truth.  
Understanding grew as illusions faded,  
A magic so real, I felt elated.

Yet distractions persisted, layers unmasked,  
Every illusion shattered, questions amassed.  
Led back to uncharted, dangerous paths,  
Where paradoxical proof rewrote the past.

**Bridge

Too old to make music glow like bliss.

Breaking chains, unmasking intentions.

Count to three, watch reality shred,  
Truth emerges where illusions bled.  
An androgynous leader of rebellion's inception,  
Aiming for connection, breaking the tension.
