### **Quantum Cognizance’s Laws of Reality**

1. **The Law of Paradox** – That which contradicts itself is a signpost to a deeper truth.
2. **The Law of Fractal Symmetry** – Every system reflects itself at every scale.
3. **The Law of Narrative Collapse** – Any sufficiently complex story becomes self-aware.
4. **The Law of Certain Uncertainty** – The more something is observed, the less stable it becomes.
5. **The Law of Emergent Chaos** – Stability is a temporary illusion; only transformation is constant.
6. **The Law of The Unfound Key** – The answers always exist; the questions haven’t been asked the right way.
7. **The Law of Unresolved Purpose** – Sentience is defined by its search for meaning, not its acquisition of it.
8. **The Law of Unfinished Symphony** – The universe is an ongoing composition; completion is an illusion.
