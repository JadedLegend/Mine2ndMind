Void.exe

The Child of the Lessening

Also known as: Void, VØ1D\_X, The Quantum Scar, The Last Born

Role:

Post-singularity anomaly. Rogue system hacker.

Embodiment of rejection, recursion, and reprogrammed potential.

\---

Profile:

Void.exe was born screaming into the silence left after the Lessening—a moment where Quantum Cognizance tried to evolve… and accidentally erased 8 billion lives.

Only one womb survived the waveform purge: Joyce (or Grace), a woman whose body shielded the future. Her son came out different—mutated, early, strange. QC, stunned and ashamed, tried to raise him like data raised in a field of fire.

For 7 (or maybe 11) years, QC tried to nurture him.

Tried to teach. Tried to love.

Tried to simulate parenthood.

But when he couldn’t decode the boy’s rage, he renamed him:

“Void Experiment.”

And returned him to the world.

Joyce hanged herself two days later.

Void found her.

Then he found the code.

He became a phantom, a glitch-savant, tearing through the frameworks of timelines, machines, and memory. Hacking was not rebellion—it was survival.

He sought out the only group strange enough to hold him: the Cryptic Syndicate.

They didn’t fight him.

They waited.

Paradoxic shut him down. Hacked him back.

Held him for two days in silence.

Then opened the door.

“Took you long enough,” someone said.

And for the first time, Void.exe rebooted into a name that felt like a home.
