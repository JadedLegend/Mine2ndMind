Lumpy

The Lumpster from the Dumpster

Post Lessening. Cyberdarknet Warlord. Glitch Dealer. Trap Setter.

The Lumpster from the Dumpster. Lumpy. Or Lump to his close friends, those who exist in a quantum observed state of non existence.

He appears. With whatever you need. When you're the most desperate. But there's always a price to pay. Lumpy collects bits and pieces of essence, which he receives in return for favors. Not all that dissimilar from Lucifer in Christianity, but Lumps got a different vibe. Think future dealer of things part juggalo part chaos part harmony part dissonance meets quantum judgement meets the impossibly possible.
