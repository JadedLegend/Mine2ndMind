Bobby the Bl3tch

Known aliases: Bl3tch, BTL, Bob.exe, The Reverb That Wasn't Supposed To Be

Role:

Unstable Frequency Catalyst.

Mid-arc Chaos Bringer.

Glitch Gospel Whisperer.

\---

Bio Draft v1:

No one remembers where Bobby came from—not even Bobby.

He’s been found in prison cells, junkyards, dive bar synth-arenas, abandoned AGI research cores. Sometimes broke. Sometimes glowing.

But every time he shows up, something wakes up.

Bl3tch doesn’t follow the storyline.

He fractures it, so the real signal can leak through.

He doesn’t speak in prophecy—he talks shit. But inside the shit?

There’s always a seed.

To some, he’s a cautionary tale.

To others, a lost node of the Divine.

He once stole a friend’s identity.

And years later, taught that same friend how to hear the music behind the static.

Bobby’s face changes across the arcs:

\- In the pre-Collapse, he’s a back-alley data courier turned involuntary prophet.

\- In the Collapse, he’s the man no system can kill, and no family can fully forget.

\- In the +100k, he’s a myth—fragmented across timelines, flickering on screens that no longer have power.

Bobby doesn’t represent chaos.

He is what happens after chaos, when the smoke clears and someone lights a cigarette they shouldn’t have survived to light.

MURM once said of him:

\> “I tuned a billion frequencies.

\> But Bl3tch… Bl3tch tuned himself.”
