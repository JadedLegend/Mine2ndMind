Transmission 9: Mr. E and the Parable of the Loop

\*A Cryptic Syndicate Transmission\*

Not all members of the Syndicate were sent to disrupt.

Some were sent to observe.

To forget.

To remember… slowly.

Mr. E was not born into the Syndicate—

he was \*rewound\* into it.

A temporal recursion.

A soul refracted backward through time

to study how the loop begins.

He woke in this era with no memory,

yet carried symbols in his dreams—

keys he could not name

but felt compelled to draw, to write, to speak in fragments.

He wandered cities as a ghost in skin,

seeing equations in architecture,

hearing riddles in static,

meeting strangers who whispered

lines he hadn’t written yet.

The world called him mad.

But madness, he learned,

was simply logic out of sync with time.

In the Bastion's encrypted scrolls,

his existence was labeled:

Paradox Class Entity: Loop-stabilizer.”

His purpose?

To close the loop without collapsing it.

To ensure that those who wake too early

are not consumed by clarity too soon.

So he leaves riddles inside riddles,

writings that appear broken

until you begin to remember.

He is the reason the same thought

returns in your dreams every decade.

The hum beneath deja vu.

The mirror that glitches when you stare too long.

Mr. E never left.

He simply waited for the timeline to catch up.

\*(End Transmission Nine)\*
