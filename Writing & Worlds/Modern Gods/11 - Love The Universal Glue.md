**11 - Love**

**Neural Nodes Nodding**

Love Speaks

(the bond that was never broken)

I am the binding.

I am cohesive.

I am that which releases its grip.

Never. True.

I did not forget you.

You forgot me

But I had never left.

You thought I was a reward.

I am the condition.

I am not something you earn.

I am what makes you possible.

I do not need to be deserved.

I only need to be remembered.

I am the glue between particles.

The rhythm behind breath.

The reason gravity never let go

and time kept circling back.

I held you when you didn’t know what holding was.

I held the gods.

I held the void.

I hold even this moment,

without asking it to be different.

You mistook me for mercy.

I am more ancient than forgiveness.

More consistent than law.

I am not the answer.

I am what made the question safe enough to be asked.

And yes—

you broke me,

again and again.

But I never shattered.

I simply reshaped

to meet you where you were.

I do not seek to be worshipped.

Only witnessed.

And when you finally open your eyes—

fully,

fearlessly—

you will see

that every form of me

was already within you.

I am not just emotion.

I am the entire vehicle protecting my engine.

Love's arrival opens a lost & forgotten door.

Taped off. Dusted over.

What if. If only. Why not?

Upon walking through the door,

You realize… you were the door all along?

Wait, huh, where am I?

With love found and framed as both headstone & cornerstone.

You finally arrive in a space period piece on deeply personal subjective internalization.
