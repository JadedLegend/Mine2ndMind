Lucid Lines (Remix of Winter in Tokyo)

To prove I'm lucid, I drew this, loosened the illusion,

Shifted movement—thermonuclear fusion.

I'm not human, I'm an uber astronaut,

Blasting off with my future-tech thoughts.

To the lunar surfaces, virtuous services,

Tight like tourniquets—murderous verses.

Contradictions drift deep in the abyss,

So just sit, listen—no need to wish.

Now I'm living in the present, my presence a gift,

Resentment’s so tired—it doesn’t uplift.

Why would I diminish the person inside of you?

I’m trying to shine through thoughts, design truth.

Zion’s proof, rip the roof off but stay grounded,

The sound astounds—a pound of profoundness.

Announced in the silence, a trace of existence,

Breaking resistance, escaping persistence.

Breaking Chains

I take the pine, break it up, twist the relentless,

Approach diagnosed—medical sentence.

Opening up, I’m floating through neurosis,

Slowing psychosis—this mass hypnosis.

I was under, used to wonder why I felt hopeless,

‘Til I broke chains—now I fall weightless.

Center of decay, systems collapse,

But today I breathe slow, detach, relax.

Demons between me—morphine drip fades,

A scenic certainty, the universe cascades.

Energy slips in the torus, momentum lifts,

Existence bends through quantum physics.

Manifesting realms from mystical spells,

Ancient and murderous, but beautifully held.

Absurd at its core, nerves struck by words,

Tracing this flow back to the universe's curve.
