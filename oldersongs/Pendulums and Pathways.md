Energy coursing through my meridians,

Exemplary synergy respecting the equilibrium,

Like a pendulum, and it’s never done,

There’s no finish line—live sublime beneath the sun.

Become the only one to influence the movement of emotion,

The truth is a blueprint, used with intention for ascension.

Break free from the fake things—fear and hate,

That pull heartstrings into darkness, hardening fate.

I’m pardoning past transgressions, part of the plan and the lesson,

Depression had me second-guessing my internal universe.

But now, it’s a true rebirth.

I’m doing dirt, planting plants, dancing, and chilling—

A villain amassing a village, slashing madness with balanced abstractness.

Not your average gymnastics with a backflip,

The track slips, but I catch it—this is my canvas, and I respect it.

The question is: what’s the quest my message points toward?

I’m one with the best, no destroying that chord.

It’s disappointing that my pointy hat and cane’ll wrack your brain.

I’m after change, blasting campaigns,

Outlasting disdain.

I think that grain may have domesticated us,

Seems this way, because before planting seeds,

We needed no money, just what we found each day.

A gatherer of points of view, a few disasters got me through.

The door resisted, I persisted, like a dork avoiding pork,

But damn, that shit is delicious.

I’m vicious like Sid is, but on blotter paper.

Got a lot of haters, rising like elevators.

Educators and creators studying behaviors,

Truth players paving ancient pathways—

Dangerous without the right direction.

Don’t mess without some apprehension.

Respecting traditions, visions gifted within a mission of truth,

Religion is movement.

I move with swiftness, a kickflip into a sick trip,

Get back up, let the cut infect, this is my medicine.

I’m better than I’ve ever been.

Pretending with penmanship perfected like peppermints,

Ice cold—Mike Jones, smoking bones outside nursing homes,

Always in my zone.
