I do not one bit trust this current system,  
Locked in, forced to exist within its prism.  
Since my nascency, I’ve persistently tried,  
Through deadly repetition, to make sense of its lies.

But instead of the praise I deserve to hear,  
Humbled like any bird that soars the atmosphere,  
I’m shouting truths, counteracting their acts,  
Hoping to retract until everyone grasps  
That absolutes are rarely true—they’re an art, intact,  
Even long after their metaphysical impacts.

Delving into darkness, they launch attacks,  
The largest ever recorded, no turning back.  
According to my documentation of history,  
The victors spread their vicious renditions with consistency.

Treating equals as less than, dividing the masses—  
I’m tired of seeing this through shattered glasses.  
Don’t believe it? Open a book, take a closer look—  
The mission’s clear, the truth’s everywhere they’ve cooked.

Our existence thrives in cognitive dissonance,  
Fed just enough to fuel their persistence.  
Inconsistencies turn incontrovertible,  
As power corrupts and their lies grow irreversible.

They twist the truth, sell contradiction as vision,  
Claiming clarity while drowning in division.
