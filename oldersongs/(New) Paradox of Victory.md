Astral Flow

Puzzle pieces fit into distant arrangements,

Discover beliefs to split through changing statements.

I’m trying to find better words, clever ways to say

What similes can’t express—sentiments serve as clay.

Certainly, this excrement serves me in some unseen way,

Too obvious, or maybe I’m blessed and cursed each day.

My brain rhymes in cold spines that shine—

Nonsensical, yet I float along this solar ocean line.

I’m hoping to break it open, see the inside,

Mind flies astral as I let dimensions collide.

Prevention enters exits through over-under apprenticeships,

Meant this when I said it, a merit in every slip.

I’ve got enough for myself and none for another,

Rediscover the mystery buried under cover.

Apparently, misery’s my shadow twin—

When I’m unplugged spiritually, it drags me in.

Struggling to connect, what the fuck is this resistance?

Persistence feels incongruent with the fluid truth’s assistance.

It pokes its head, shows me light, then leaves me blind in the cave—

Darkness bound, behaving like a devil who can’t be saved.

According to him, I’ll never win, cursed by dark magic—

My life’s been tragic, chasing distractions, manic.

Another one to dull the pain; it’s truer than the lies we’ve lived—

Belief, a fragile thing uncovered when we sift through the abyss.

I’m aligned with nothing, just floating purposeless.

My heart hurts; the truth burns, a hollow furnace.

Ripping off the roof to cough up a lung,

Vaping PCP as my epitome’s spun.

Paradox of Victory

A losing victory, paradox in motion,

I’m falling through rabbit holes, drowning in emotion.

The underground sound found in my tragic hold,

Years spent battling fears, now my mind’s grown cold.

For every step forward, I step two back,

Truth or lie, it’s subjective in this mental attack.

Shining like a diamond in the rough, I’m kinda fucked up—

Nine designer drugs, defiant thugs.

Grinding alkaline, defiant love, and trust—

Know you cannot touch us because we adjust.

We’re holographic, mathematic in nature,

Acrobatic with how I flip the track and paper.

Born from hate and scorn,

I take warmth to the outer limits—weathering the storm.

Patching this torn meniscus while I storm the entrance,

Warn the minions, but mountains won’t move with pretense.

Uncounted, I found it, underground soundless,

This palace of hidden challenge leaves me astounded.

The torn fabric stitched with the essence of balance—

I channel it now, carrying the weightless malice.
