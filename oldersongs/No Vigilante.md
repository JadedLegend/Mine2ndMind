I’m no vigilante or phantom,  
This is nothing more than my anti-anthem.  
Done a whole lot of not planning,  
Landed me in lockup—life demanding.

Institutionalized since I was fifteen,  
Through my late twenties, it’s all I’ve seen.  
Life’s been funny—if funny means pain,  
Like laughing with a knife twisting veins.

Cooking up peace, but it’s always runny,  
Trade me your right leg—don’t act so sunny.  
A starving thespian, no audience to admit,  
They ask me, “Has that got you shit?”

It’s depressing to think where the party went,  
Chased the vibes, but it hollowed my intent.  
Can I borrow fifty bucks ‘til tomorrow?  
Go ahead and weep—sorrow’s the motto.

Try to reach me? No comunicado.  
Thoughts circling tight, trapped in a grotto.  
When I’m rolling ‘round the rink, I think—  
It ain’t drink; it’s the shadows I link.

No flow I spit will breach the gatekeepers’ walls,  
Surrounding me’s the unseen weight that calls.  
For years, I thought I might be in a dream,  
Reality flickers, but nothing’s as it seems.

They wonder how I’ve grown, what stacks I’ve amassed,  
But my growth ain’t cash—it’s spells I’ve cast.  
Words are magic, turbo-charged like a battery,  
Paradoxical as me, contradiction naturally.

Surrounding me, grounding me—it’s all so deep.  
I once held peace for a moment to keep.  
The past shadows me, its echoes I reap,  
While the future’s a mystery I can’t quite meet.

I’m slow, drawn to lo-fi and low-pro,  
Distractions stack like questions I don’t wanna know.  
Honestly, I tether THC straight to my veins—  
If you could see what I see, you’d unshackle your chains.

But no one listens, no curiosity sparked,  
Maddening audacity—ideas left in the dark.  
Sores bleed truths we refuse to explore,  
The pus, the ooze, the rot at the core.

It’s boring to scream at a world that’s snoring,  
But I’ll keep spitting—truth’s worth exploring.
