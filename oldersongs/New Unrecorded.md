Listen to this—I’m begging for one wish,  
Leave it, or my music will again become paralyzed, dismissed.  
Don’t pretend it’s not from atrophy,  
As I open pages endlessly, filled back and front with literal literature, vividly.

These words were gifted to me from the year 4043,  
But nobody ever stops, thinks, and then listens to my plea.  
My mission sits in timeless compositions,  
Yet I’m lost in opportunities I keep on missing.

Given a surge of certainty, I’m feeling reborn,  
More of myself revealed, like a rose with no thorn.  
But something feels missing—a hook, a chorus, a spark for us.  
Should I add it, to make the forest of my art more lush?

Terrified of my self-consciousness,  
Trying to close the gaps, but leave it less ominous.  
I’m hooked into this instrumental sound,  
Digging deep underground, wondering what’s allowed.

But for once, I feel happy and proud,  
Accepting the objective, moving forward unbowed.  
I’m messing with producers—pleasant and effervescent—  
And I’ll always give it back, make their names luminescent.

Why wouldn’t I bless others who help me discover,  
The way out of this tunnel, where shadows hover?  
My mind bends like light in a bubble,  
Locked in trouble but strong enough to muscle the struggle.

Humans and their truth leave me reduced,  
As I uncover roots they’ve severed and abused.  
I wonder—are my words pointless in this digital age?  
AI isn’t the problem; it’s the stage we’ve set for rage.

I’m not quite certain, but I feel the curtain,  
And I’m hurting, hoping someone sees my purpose is urgent.  
So I better start living and loving,  
Before uncertainty becomes my only subject of discussion.
