**Verse 1  
**Writing like a titan, you know I’m tightening the grip,  
Slipping into visions I’m designing—thanks for vibing with this.  
It isn’t what I thought it was; the love comes from above,  
And it fits me like a glove—I’m learning to trust the process.

To be honest, I’ve made a lot of progress,  
Wrestling with complex concepts,  
Swept contempt for myself beneath the shelf,  
Made amends with it—my penmanship speaks for itself.

I’m running, helping, melting what no longer serves me, urgently  
Releasing beliefs, finding peace and tranquility.  
I’m chillin’, freezing abilities, spilling pieces of secrets—  
Dreaming of the Zenith, complete with telekinesis.

My dexterity’s a rarity, brimming with clarity and patience,  
Destined to dissect density, potentially uncovering levitation.  
Unfolding through meditation, resisting changes  
In this strange shift—death and rebirth in phases.

These three horns, metaphorical, as I let the forest grow,  
Over my soul, learning to let go while respecting the flow.  
It moves through my bones, my home away from home,  
I’ve been shown ugliness, but I’ve grown beyond it alone.

Melting the vision that causes restriction—  
This is an honest rendition of what I’ve been missing.  
Living in the future, rediscovering the present—  
Every lesson reflects the essence of resilience.

**Verse 2  
**Merge through into a new room in this mental house,  
Letting out a strange amount of music—the truth is, it’s therapeutic.  
I need it—belief’s a double-edged truth,  
A lie at its best, as I rest beneath the Bodhi tree.

I’m only me—lonely, but we’re in therapy.  
Apparently, my mental state’s irate, laced with self-hate,  
But I’m working through the traumas—some days easier than others.  
Rediscovering myself, becoming my own lover.

Flutter towards the light like a moth, illusions aren’t right,  
So I write to pierce through them. Truth’s more than this movement.  
Shooting with precision, I’m giving a true description,  
Moving through dimensions, preventing inhibition.

Touching divinity, beautifully, let it be for infinity.  
Grilling cheese—vegan, undecided.  
Dumb and high kid? No, that’s not me—oh, it is though.  
I’m everything, bro.

Becoming more as I keep growing—opening my mind and soul,  
Taking sovereign control.  
Can’t spell that, but I felt that—  
It’s selfless, not selfish. I help with the song,  
It moves through my soul, but when I stop to look, it’s gone.

My mind takes control, messing it up like, “What’s up, though?  
You’re smart, but not as smart as me—I’m the master.”  
But I release all ownership of self, transcend  
Physical limitations, and ascend reality itself.
