Astral Visions

Let me explain in a way your brain can ascertain,

The story of how I came down from outer space now.

As I vape this isolated tryptamine to break the tessellated,

Mentally created computer screen—it’s overcomplicated.

But see, the dreams are too much to count,

A great amount if you knew what they’re about.

Cookin’ DMT so easily with some simple chemistry,

We freebased it, escaped it, and faced infinity.

Stimulated energy, blending synergistically,

Coalescing slowly, unlocking infinity’s mystery.

Delivered me directly into the heart of imagery,

Melting into the universal symphony.

We replayed it, arrangement evaluated,

The placement of amazement—a maze that’s man-created.

Yes, I made this in my spaceship, an honest statement,

About the crazed amazement that’s displayed to escape pain quick.

Redirect your course to discover the source,

Of the endless wars—we’re lovers and fighters at our core.

Blessed with introspection, I force this connection,

To guide you forth into the fourth dimension.

My repertoire is bizarre, but it’s hardly hard to catch,

With a large reservoir of attention, here’s the match:

Add through subtraction, subtract through addiction,

Quiet ambition, loud with a wise vision.

Try to listen as I drip rhythm through the treble,

Settle like a pebble underneath the water level.

Exponential graffiti with existential strokes,

Painting truths in the shadows, waking sleeping folks.

Deep in the shallows, believe me, you’ll find me,

Hiding in alcoves, ceremonial tobacco binds me.

This astral projection isn’t a fleeting reflection,

It’s a map to the cosmos, a multidimensional connection.
