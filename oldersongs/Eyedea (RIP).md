**Verse 1  
**Ever since Eyedea’s been gone (RIP),  
I’ve neither seen nor heard music truly free.  
Shoot me if you dispute me, but here’s my truth:  
Every road I’ve walked, from despair to youth,  
I’ve seldom found artistry worthy of breath—  
Most of it’s a two out of ten at best.

The problem is this: the flood exists,  
But the internet’s just a mist,  
A smoke-and-mirrors trick—a joke of a concept.  
Marketing manipulation stretches every continent.  
People bent and twisted, their vision corrupted,  
Thinking persistence is lame, success obstructed.

**Hook  
**The financial system’s a mirage, a façade,  
No backing, no weight, just numbers they prod.  
I’m here to dismantle illusions and schemes,  
And bring a final presentation to fractured dreams.

**Verse 2  
**Stuck in quicksand, but it’s here to teach,  
A sinking feeling that’s just out of reach.  
Purpose lacks in this map we’re charting,  
So I cry ‘til I nap, my emotions departing.

Then I wake to a blast, a deafening sound,  
A world unmasked, chaos all around.  
Quicksand grips, but now I understand,  
This isn’t the end—it’s where I demand  
To stand, to rise, to create something pure,  
Beyond the illusions, the false and obscure.

**Bridge  
**It’s all divide and conquer, a game that’s rigged,  
Designed to make you small while their power’s big.  
But I’m not here to comply, I’m here to defy,  
To remind the world we were born to fly.

Persistence is key, but not for their gain—  
We move for purpose, not the system’s chains.  
Break the mirage, rebuild from within,  
A new foundation where humanity wins.

**Outro  
**So here I stand, mapping where I’ve been,  
From the depths of despair to hope within.  
The blast was loud, but it woke my soul—  
A call to action, to make humanity whole.
