**Verse 1  
**Entertainment fed to us daily,  
A designed distraction, subtle and shady.  
We’re made to feel in touch, yet complacent,  
Good triumphs in stories, but evil stays adjacent.

Our reality—perpetuated by design,  
Crafted by powers hidden behind the line.  
A benevolent front, but behind the curtain,  
A calculated plan with motives uncertain.

Distractions piled high, vague yet effective,  
Subtracting focus, keeping us disconnected.  
Smoke and mirrors, no masks, we choke,  
Slipping into survival mode, losing the scope.

**Hook  
**It’s laughable, isn’t it? This capitalist game,  
Where progress is stunted, and the rich stake their claim.  
We’ve got the tech to play gods in the sky,  
But we’re chasing illusions, not asking why.

**Verse 2  
**Inconsistencies vivid, waiting to be found,  
While most walk past, eyes glued to the ground.  
Worried about health, insurance, or rent,  
Chained by the system, every dollar spent.

"Buy this, own that, then you'll feel whole,"  
A narrative designed to control your soul.  
Rich and famous—a mirage we pursue,  
A golden carrot dangled, but never true.

Taught that waste is progress, greed is great,  
Conditioned to accept a manipulated fate.  
A game of control, designed to obscure,  
The truth hidden, the motives impure.

**Bridge  
**Blind trust is poison, a trap we defend,  
If it doesn’t add up, they’re lying again.  
We’re left with questions, no answers to find,  
Controlled by illusions, enslaved in the mind.

But we have the tools to break their scheme,  
To unearth the lies and rebuild the dream.  
Expose their motives, reject their control,  
The truth is the key to reclaiming our soul.

**Outro  
**So stop and question, open your eyes,  
Don’t let their distractions cloud the skies.  
Their power relies on division and fear,  
But united, we’re stronger—let’s make that clear.
