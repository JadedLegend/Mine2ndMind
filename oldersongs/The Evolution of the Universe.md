The Evolution of the Universe

To prove I’m lucid, I drew this

To loosen the illusion and move into thermonuclear fusion.

I’m not a human—I’m an uber astronaut,

A traveler from the future, blasting off with my computer.

To the lunar surfaces, my virtuous services

Are tight like tourniquets when I murder this.

It contradicts, drifts deep into the abyss,

So just sit, listen—there’s no more wishin’.

I’m livin’ in the present, my presence is a present.

Resentment’s so uninventive—think critically for a second.

Why would I diminish the person inside of you?

I’m trying to design a group of thoughts to shine it through,

Like the Zion truth, the highest proof.

Rip the roof off and stay grounded,

The sound is astounding—a pound or an ounce.

I found my announcement hidden in the silence,

A cosmic vibration, a timeless alignment.

So now I trace the outline of existence,

Trying to break my mind out of this resistance.

I take the pine, break it up, twist it—

A relentless approach, a medical diagnosis.

As I’m opening, I’m floating in neurosis.

I really need to focus, closing in, slowing this psychosis.

Mass hypnosis had me under, wondering why I felt so hopeless.

Then I broke this ball and chain—now I fall away,

To the center of the system, where decay makes way for creation.

Today, I lean into slow breathing,

Not believing the demons—I sit between them.

A morphine drip eases the pain of this scenic certainty.

It’s seen so simply, universally, as I ascend intentionally.

Learning of the energy slips within the torus,

Momentum lifts existence through quantum forces.

It manifests in the physical realm,

A mystical spell that bridges the helm.

Words hit a nerve—absurd, murderous, and sick,

Yet beautiful and ancient, truths that stick.

This is the evolution of the universe, unfurled,

A paradoxical symphony, rebirthing the world.
