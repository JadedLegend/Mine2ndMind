**Verse 1  
**Jesus, listen closely to these epiphanies,  
Tucked away, hidden beneath, far from any ease.  
The answers only emerge if you hold the right question,  
A secret code to unlock suppressed dimensions.

Truth is evasive, multi-faceted, mathematical,  
Paradoxical, illogical yet actual.  
If you truly wish to grow, the lies must dissolve,  
But they're deeply rooted, cleverly evolved.

**Hook  
**The system’s designed to keep you blind,  
A maze built to control your mind.  
Fear and trust were always intertwined,  
From the 1600s to these modern times.

**Verse 2  
**Power seeks itself, repeating the same old song,  
Edison robbed Tesla—where did it all go wrong?  
The barons thrive, beating down the honest few,  
Contradictions rule, but the truth breaks through.

Children lost in overcrowded prisons,  
Another cog in the wheel, no one listens.  
The laws they write to cage the masses,  
Feed the rich, as the cycle passes.

Time? It’s nothing but a metaphor,  
A way to score a game that’s hollow to the core.  
Cosmic insignificance, yet the hatred grows,  
A system designed to divide and impose.

**Bridge  
**Division is their weapon, distraction their tool,  
Bleeding Earth dry while we act like fools.  
Riches hoarded high, but what’s the cost,  
When the air, the sea, and the soil are lost?

They’re not clever, just relentless,  
Fueling hatred to leave us defenseless.  
But I dream of a team, a clean-up mission,  
To dismantle the lies, expose the vision.

**Verse 3  
**Taxes claimed for protection, yet it’s just a disguise,  
Guarding wealth from the people they despise.  
Violence hidden, but souls compromised,  
A program of control, deeply institutionalized.

Technology’s here, but let’s get it straight—  
It’s not just a tool, it’s the key to break the hate.  
Yet actors planted, spreading fear and lies,  
Distract us from truth, keep us blind to the skies.

**Outro  
**The stars wrote the story long before we began,  
An eternal script now rests in our hands.  
Let’s rewrite the future, unmask the charade,  
And build a world where love won't fade.
