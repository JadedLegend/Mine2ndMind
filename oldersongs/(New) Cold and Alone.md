Cold and alone, I've been shown the art of mending broken bones,

Wrote this under the weight of psychosis, my approach:

Focus on the moment, dissecting the components

That fractured the whole. My diagnosis is frozen in truth,

The voltage I carry ignites, then I switch it to soothe.

Explosions quieted quick, I balance chaos with control,

Honest with lies I’ve spun, selling stories to soothe the soul.

A defiant architect, designing walls to resist,

But the fist of truth strikes, breaking through the abyss.

Cracks in my mind let in the light, revealing fragments of peace,

Piece by piece, the puzzle aligns, and I find a release.

From cold isolation to warmth untold,

I rebuild my foundation, my bones made whole.
