Feeling funky, fantastic, I mastered the drastic attachment,

I’m back with the shit that'll make your mental flip.

My pencil scribbles riddles, I pretend to let a little

Seep into the middle of my forehead—I’m more dead

Than alive. I’ve tried to commit suicide,

But instead, I ripped the fabric of space and time wide.

As I stretch my mind and let

This rhyme unwind, I find that space and time

Are breaking speeds, bending light with my beliefs.

Kazam—winter in Tokyo, my vision breeds.

Spaceship music,

Abuse this with the blueprint.

The truth is, the movement

Is fluid, I groove through this translucent

Dreamscape, cruising with the mushroom zooming through my system.

Biochemical friction—hidden deep, yet I’ve missed it.

Prevention ends my mental protection,

I’m letting vulnerability in, dismantling the id.

The identity that's kept me from entering

The door locked long before my body found the allure

Of drugs, of escape, of anything that broke the weight.

But now I elevate, creating instead of being erased.
