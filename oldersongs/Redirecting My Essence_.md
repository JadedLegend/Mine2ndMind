Redirecting my essence, blessed with the best—

Testing me’s hard, no stress, just finesse.

Questions less than fleeting, like a split-second meeting

With a doctor in your head—your perception’s repeating.

I don’t need them to represent, not for me.

My entrance blocked by henchmen—do you see?

Whatever’s clever never mattered, no, really.

I’m heated up, 420 degrees, clearly.

What I’m feeling is my mind’s healing process,

A section of my consciousness intersected and connected.

Be honest for a second, watch where you’re stepping—

This immaculate biotech in your head is a weapon.

When used correctly, I teach the lesson—

I’m present, I gave you the option to end it.

But why pretend this is a friendless dimension,

When I got ten men ready for ascension?

Then they trip, they fall—heavy like cannonballs,

No man involved, solving problems evolves.

The possibility to call time’s journey unbending,

Attempting to navigate reality’s endless ending.

My habits tower too tall for me to climb them constantly.

It’s been hard for me to find a way to evolve consciously.

So I dissolved my mind in alkaline entheogens,

Forgot to pretend, sent it, and transcended.

No mention crept in on how to extend this,

It’s nothing more, nothing less than pure intention.
