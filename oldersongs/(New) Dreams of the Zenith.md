Verse 1:

Writing like a titan, you know I’m tightening the grip

Slipping into what I envision—thanks for listening to this

It isn’t what I thought it was—the love flows in from above

And fits me like a glove, I’m learning to trust the process

To be honest, I’ve made some real progress

Wrestling with complex concepts

Swept contempt for myself beneath the shelf

Made a friend of it, found strength in the depth

My penmanship is unmatched, I’m helping and melting

All that no longer serves me, urgently

Releasing beliefs, finding peace in tranquility

Chilling, freeze—abilities spilling like mysteries

I dream of Zenith, paired with telekinesis

Rarity in dexterity, clarity in patience

Destiny calls, dismantling the density

Potentially discovering levitation

Through meditation, I uncover revelations

Strange how these plane shifts bring transformations

Die and be reborn, the metaphor of three horns

Let the forest grow over my soul, take control

By letting go—respect the flow moving through my bones

It’s my home away from home, I’ve seen ugliness

I’m over this self-imposed punishment

Melt the vision causing restriction, an honest rendition

Of what was missing, living now—not the future.

Verse 2:

Merge through into new rooms of the mental house

I’m letting out streams of sound—the truth is therapeutic

I need it—belief is truth, but a lie at best

I rest beneath the bodhi tree, unraveling me

A little lonely, see? But this is therapy

My mental state oscillates—irate with a side of self-hate

But I’m working through trauma, finding calm where drama dwells

Rediscovering myself, becoming my own lover

Flutter toward the light, a moth seeking clarity

Illusions fade as I write, words untangling disparity

Truth moves deeper than a single movement

Shoot with precision, offering true descriptions

To shift dimensions, touching divinity

It’s beautifully infinite—let it be

Grilling cheese, vegan though—undecided

Dumb and high? No, but maybe—it’s all inside me

Everything bro, and I’ll become more as I grow

Opening mind and soul to claim control sovereignly

I may not spell it right, but I feel it tight—

It isn’t selfish, it’s selfless, as I help with the song

That moves through my essence—I pause to reflect

But my mind takes over, disrupts, and interjects

Like, “Whaddup though, you’re smart, but not as smart as me”

I release ownership of self, transcend physicality

Limitations of reality dissolve, I revolve

Through patterns of thought that no longer evolve

And now, I’m free to embrace what’s meant to be

A vessel for the infinite energy inside of me.
