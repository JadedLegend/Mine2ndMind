The Drip Stability Protocol

(a ritual for learning how to keep what flows)

1\. Arrival

When money appears—cash, a deposit, a found coin—pause.

Hold it in your hand or imagine its weight.

Breathe once in through your nose, once out through your mouth.

Say quietly:

\> “I recognize this arrival.”

Don’t calculate yet. Don’t plan.

Just notice that energy has entered your orbit and you are still safe.

\---

2\. Containment (The 24-Hour Seal)

Do nothing for a single rotation of the earth.

Let the energy settle instead of disperse.

This is the nervous system learning that keeping does not equal corruption.

If you feel restless, write instead of spend:

\> “I could use this for \_\_\_, and still be okay if I don’t.”

\---

3\. The Drip Reserve

Keep one container—jar, pocket, folder, digital sub-account—named The Drip Reserve.

Each time something arrives, even one dollar joins that pool.

Don’t touch it for thirty days.

It’s a living metaphor that flow can gather without dying.

\---

4\. Reciprocal Breath

At least once a day, practice receiving something non-monetary.

Let it land completely—a smile, a beam of sun, a song that finds you.

Whisper:

\> “I allow this in.”

This builds the same circuitry that receives money without recoil.

\---

5\. Micro-Release

Once a week, give intentionally—a tip, a favor, a small leak.

Do it consciously:

\> “This is not escape. This is circulation.”

The act reminds the body that release can be chosen, not compelled.

\---

6\. Attunement Journal

Track only one thing: the felt sense of enough.

Morning and night, name it in a sentence:

\> “Right now, enough feels like \_\_\_.”

Over weeks, you’ll begin to notice safety existing at more than one dollar amount.

\---

7\. Closing the Loop

On the last day of each month, open The Drip Reserve.

Observe what stayed.

Whether it’s $3 or $30, don’t spend it immediately.

Touch it. Thank it.

Say:

“Retention is not resistance.”

Then decide, intuitively, how much to keep in the reserve and how much to release.
