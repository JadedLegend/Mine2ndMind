**Types of Paradoxes: Logical, Semantic, and Existential**

Paradoxes manifest in various forms, each challenging our understanding in distinct ways. **Logical paradoxes**, such as Russell's paradox, expose inherent contradictions within formal systems or deductive reasoning, often pointing to fundamental limits in logic itself. **Semantic paradoxes**, like the Liar Paradox ("This statement is false"), arise from self-referential statements within language, demonstrating how meaning and truth can become entangled and self-defeating. Finally, **existential paradoxes** delve into the inherent contradictions of human experience, identity, and consciousness, frequently explored in philosophy and literature, highlighting the tensions between freedom and determinism, or meaning and absurdity. Understanding these distinct categories is crucial for appreciating the multifaceted role of paradox in revealing the boundaries of knowledge across diverse disciplines.
