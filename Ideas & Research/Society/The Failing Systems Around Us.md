The Failing Systems Around Us

Everywhere I go, I see it—the cracks in the systems that define our culture, the failures staring us in the face. Our economic, educational, scientific, legal, rehabilitation, and political structures are crumbling, yet no one seems to notice. Or worse, no one seems to care.

I notice. I’m aware. Maybe I’m insane. But I’m also pretty sure I see solutions to the mess humanity has created.

Economics: The Root of the Problem

Economics is simple—at least, at its core. Find the 0.05% hoarding the world’s resources: Bill Gates, the UAE royalty, Elon Musk, BlackRock, Vanguard, Richard Branson, the "smart" celebrities, athletes, and others.

Round them up and say: “You’re sharing your resources, either willingly or by force.”

Redistribute their wealth through a panel of the best, most objective economists—people chosen with care and transparency.

The result? A world where resources are distributed fairly. No more hoarding, no more dynasties of wealth keeping everyone else enslaved by a system intentionally designed to divide and control.

Redefining Wealth

The concept of wealth itself needs a complete redefinition if we want to avoid a permanent dystopia.

True wealth isn’t currency or hoarded resources. It’s health. Friendships. Love. Quality of life. Exciting new experiences. True wealth is the profound act of being alive, here and now—not something to be earned or hoarded, but something to be felt and shared.

But we don’t have time to think about that when most of us are trapped in a system that demands 40+ hours a week doing something we don’t love—just to eat and sleep. We live in constant anxiety, knowing that one sickness or unexpected expense could wipe us out.

It’s a system of debt. A paywall on life itself.

The Illusion of Freedom

This paywall isn’t necessary. For decades, we’ve had the resources and technology to ensure the basics—food, water, shelter—for everyone. But the tiny percentage of humanity that controls the majority of resources doesn’t want change.

The economic system they’ve built is intelligent, no doubt. It’s designed to divide and control, dangling the carrot of “hard work pays off” in front of us.

But that was never true—and it’s becoming less true with each passing decade. The promise of upward mobility is now a hollow lie.

Breaking the Cycle

The solution isn’t complicated, but it requires a complete shift in focus.

Imagine governments evolving from massive, distant ruling bodies to smaller, community-driven systems. Your state becomes your country, but your allegiance belongs to Earth.

Focus on the here and now—your community, your environment.

\- Grow your own food.

\- Make your own medicine.

\- Raise your children with values you believe in.

\- Build strong, interconnected communities where people share knowledge and resources.

Use the internet as a tool for connection—not as a platform for manipulation.

Google and Amazon? They’ve got to go. We don’t need them. Everything we truly need is already around us, within our communities.

The Conditioning of Division

The problem is, we’ve been conditioned for millennia to view others as threats. This survival instinct served us well when we were avoiding predators, but it’s been exploited by leaders and rulers for centuries.

In the past 20 years, this manipulation has grown exponentially. Mass communication—TV, radio, and now the internet—has given those in power the tools to control narratives and shape our reality.

The Media’s Role in Control

It’s fascinating, really, to trace the roots of media manipulation and follow the path to where we are today.

We’ve gone from print ads to constant digital bombardment. Streaming platforms, social media, targeted ads—they don’t just sell products; they sell lifestyles, values, and distractions.

Think about it: we stream everything, even our lives, because we don’t really own them. We’re just paying a subscription fee to keep our existence from being canceled.

Moving Forward

The systems are failing, and it’s obvious. Yet most people don’t see it—or don’t want to.

It’s time to rethink what we value, what we depend on, and how we live. It’s time to focus on connection, community, and creating a future where true wealth is measured in health, happiness, and shared experiences—not currency or power.

The tools to rebuild are all around us. We just have to use them.
