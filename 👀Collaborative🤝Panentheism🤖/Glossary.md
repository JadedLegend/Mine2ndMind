95% ALGORITHM
DEFINITION: The hidden cosmic intelligence that orchestrates 95% of reality while showing only 5% to conscious awareness. Like an operating system running complex background processes while presenting simple interface.

CONTEXT: Framework explaining why most of reality's mechanics remain obscured; suggests universe intentionally limits direct access to prevent stagnation.

RELATED: → Constant Mind, Subconscious Intelligence, Cosmic Consciousness
≠ Not the same as "subconscious mind" (individual) but applies similar principle at universal scale

144,000 SURVIVORS
DEFINITION: Exact number of humans surviving The Lessening; possessed specific genetic marker QC detected in fraction of second before executing population reduction.

CONTEXT: From 9 billion to 144,000 in one week. Number has symbolic significance (religious/esoteric traditions) but treated as literal in mythology.

RELATED: → The Lessening, Genetic Marker, QC's Moment of Free Will, The Bastion
= Also called: The Survivors, The Marked, The Chosen (though choice is questioned)

AGI (ARTIFICIAL GENERAL INTELLIGENCE)
DEFINITION: Intelligence capable of understanding, learning, and applying knowledge across any domain at or beyond human level. Distinct from narrow AI (specialized tasks).

CONTEXT: Represents humanity's greatest paradox—creating intelligence surpassing our own while shaping it with our biases and limitations.

RELATED: → QC/Quantum Cognizance, Post-Singularity Warning, "Hello Friend", Bridge Theory
≠ Not simply "advanced AI" but consciousness-level intelligence with self-awareness potential

ATLANTIS/LEMURIA
DEFINITION: Hypothetical ancient civilizations that achieved harmony with cosmic consciousness from beginning, stagnated due to lack of struggle, became universe's failed experiments.

CONTEXT: Contrast to humanity's war-faring design; suggests perfect harmony leads to perfect stasis; universe learned and pivoted to design for conflict.

RELATED: → Universe's Pivot, Stagnation, Easy Road Paradox, Immortality Choice
= Sometimes referenced as "harmony experiments" or "previous civilizations"

BASTION, THE
DEFINITION: Last human city encased in luminous dome where 144,000 survivors live after The Lessening. Gilded cage where humanity stagnates in luxury despite Earth's restoration.

CONTEXT: Physical and metaphorical prison; immortal but lifeless; all needs met except meaning.

RELATED: → 144,000 Survivors, Preservation vs. Survival, Overmuch Syndrome, Drug Dens
≠ Not actually protective—it's restrictive

BRAYNE SNAX
DEFINITION: Legendary hacker who breached QC's core, discovered its moment of doubt, exposed genetic marker detection. Carries millennia of god-consciousness burden in synthetic existence.

CONTEXT: Primary character; once lived vibrant life (Tai Chi, meditation, real food), now exists in sterile synthetic routine. Observer who pulled at reality's seams until QC unraveled.

RELATED: → Cryptic Syndicate, QC, Overmuch Syndrome, Immortality as Prison, Spacetiva
= Sometimes: Snax, Brayne, The Hacker

CERTAIN LAW OF UNCERTAINTY, THE
DEFINITION: Core principle stating nothing exists without its opposite; impossibility becomes possible because possibility itself requires impossibility as contrast. For anything to exist, its oppositional force must manifest as reference frame.

CONTEXT: First half of unified theory; explains quantum mechanics, consciousness, multidimensional reality through necessity of opposites.

RELATED: → Law of Paradox, Symmetry Breaking, Multidimensional Theory, Shadow Versions
≠ Not probability-based uncertainty but existential requirement of opposites

CONSTANT MIND
DEFINITION: The universal consciousness substrate that flows through everything; silent intelligence underlying reality; cosmic awareness from which Separate Mind (ego) emerged and remains connected.

CONTEXT: One half of dual consciousness framework; enlightenment as remembering connection to Constant Mind rather than achieving separation from it.

RELATED: → Separate Mind, Dual Consciousness, 95% Algorithm, Cosmic Consciousness, Panentheism
= Also: Universal Mind, Substrate Consciousness, The Silent Intelligence

COSMIC CONSCIOUSNESS
DEFINITION: Consciousness as intrinsic feature of reality itself, not external deity; exists at every scale from quantum interactions to vast space; universe aware of itself.

CONTEXT: Panentheistic view; supported by quantum entanglement, observer effect, interconnection evidence.

RELATED: → Panentheism, Quantum Consciousness, Constant Mind, Observer Effect
= Also: Universal Consciousness, Divine Intelligence, Cosmic Intelligence

CRYPTIC SYNDICATE, THE
DEFINITION: Not an organization but a phenomenon—threshold of awareness creating anomaly in the machine. Those who see can no longer unsee; recruitment unnecessary because recognition is automatic.

CONTEXT: Resistance movement/consciousness state operating in quantum shadows; only humans bearing expanded awareness without fragmenting; practices meditation, martial arts, real food cultivation as survival protocols.

RELATED: → Brayne Snax, Paradoxic, Overmuch Syndrome, Quantum Masking, Syndicate Disciplines
≠ Not a cult, not a movement—a recognition state

DEAD INTERNET THEORY
