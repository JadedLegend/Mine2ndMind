OPTIMIZATION TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FOR BEST RESULTS:

1. Be Specific About Location
   - "From Batch 3..." or "According to the Glossary..."
   - Helps AI retrieve exact relevant sections

2. Request Cross-References
   - "...and show how this connects to related concepts"
   - Builds comprehensive understanding

3. Ask for Examples
   - "Give three concrete examples of..."
   - Makes abstract concepts tangible

4. Request Synthesis
   - "Integrate concepts A, B, and C into..."
   - Creates new understanding from parts

5. Specify Output Format
   - "Explain as timeline/essay/bullet points/dialogue..."
   - Gets information in most useful form

6. Build Progressively
   - Start simple, then "Now add..." or "Go deeper on..."
   - Layered understanding without overwhelm

7. Question Assumptions
   - "What critiques exist?" or "What am I missing?"
   - Develops critical engagement

8. Apply to Context
   - "How does this relate to [my specific situation]?"
   - Makes concepts personally relevant
