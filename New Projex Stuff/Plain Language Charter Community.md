🌱 Plain Language Charter for Globally Spanning 1,500-Person Communities

1\. Who We Are

We are a community of about 1,500 people.

We live together, care for each other, and share responsibility for our common life.

\---

2\. Our Rights

\* Everyone has freedom of thought, speech, and choice of belief.

\* Everyone has equal access to food, water, shelter, safety, and health.

\* Everyone’s body, mind, and data belong to themselves.

\* No one may be punished or excluded without fair hearing by their peers.

\---

3\. Our Responsibilities

\* We care for the land, air, water, and life around us.

\* We respect each other’s dignity, time, and boundaries.

\* We repair harm when it happens, rather than hiding it.

\* We share what we can, and no one is left to suffer alone.

\---

4\. How We Decide

\* The community meets regularly in open assembly.

\* Anyone may bring forward a proposal.

\* Decisions are made by listening first, then seeking consensus. If consensus fails, we vote — majority rules, but minority voices are recorded and respected.

\* Leadership roles are temporary, chosen by the community, and rotated often.

\---

5\. How We Handle Resources

\* Basic needs are guaranteed before luxuries.

\* We keep a common fund for shared goods and emergencies.

\* Work and trade are fair: no one gets rich by making others poor.

\* All accounts are open for anyone to see.

\---

6\. How We Handle Conflict

\* First, we talk it out directly.

\* If that fails, a circle of peers helps mediate.

\* If harm is proven, the goal is repair and balance, not revenge.

\* If someone endangers the community and refuses repair, the assembly may limit their participation until trust is restored.

\---

7\. How We Connect to Others

\* We cooperate with neighboring communities.

\* We trade fairly, without exploitation.

\* We share knowledge, culture, and support freely.

\* We resolve disputes peacefully through dialogue and councils.

\---

8\. How We Change This Charter

\* Any member may propose a change.

\* A change requires the support of two-thirds of the community.

\* All changes must keep the rights in Section 2 intact - these cannot be taken away.

\---

9\. Our Guiding Idea

This community exists so that people may live free, safe, and fulfilled lives, while protecting each other and the world we depend on.
