### **1) The Money Ladder (mapped to your bandwidth)**

Use the thresholds you already noticed—just make them explicit:

- **$0–$5 — Auto‑OK.** Log it; no discussion needed.
- **$5–$15 — Tell.** Log it + tell Mom (a quick text).
- **$20–$100 — Ask.** Run the 3‑question litmus (below), then ask before buying.
- **\>$100 — Wait‑48h.** Log it, wait 48 hours, then you and Mom pick one: **Gift / Loan / Joint Project** (with a review date).
- **Project funding (e.g., trading):** define the project and success criteria *before* any spend.

**3‑Question Litmus**

1. Does this align with my top 3 values *right now*?
2. Will I use it ≥10 times in 10 days (or 30 for bigger buys)?
3. Will future‑me thank me for this?

## **Paradox Trader without capital (yet): a 5‑day, $0 pathway**

**Goal:** create a *visible* artifact and decision point—no heroics, no funds.

- **Day 1:** Write a 10‑sentence spec and success/failure criteria.
- **Day 2:** Backtest a *single* rule on a small historical sample (spreadsheet is fine).
- **Day 3:** Paper‑trade 10 entries; log entries/exits.
- **Day 4:** Review: hit rates, avg win/loss, max drawdown; decide to iterate or park.
- **Day 5:** If promising, continue paper trading 30 days **or** request micro‑capital with rules + stop‑loss + “shut‑off” conditions.

Use this definition of done for each step:

- One artifact someone else can see (file, link, screenshot), **and
  **
- 3–5 lines: what I tried, what happened, what I’ll try next (or why I’m parking it).

## **Routines for a freestyle mind (no rigid calendars)**

Try a **3‑Beat day** (no schedule, just beats):

- **Body (10–20 min):** walk, stretch, breath, or shower.
- **Build (17–50 min):** one block on one task (timer on, everything else off).
- **Bond (5–15 min):** one concrete act for Mom or a friend.

Two beats = a good day. Three = a great day. That’s enough.

## **Talking with your therapist about the father narrative (without being pathologized)**

Open the frame at the top of a session:

*I want to explore a working hypothesis that helps me make meaning of trauma. I’m not asking you to validate the literal story; I’m asking for help mapping how this narrative shapes my decisions, vigilance, trust, and money. I also want reality‑checks and grounding so I don’t over‑index on it.*

Use **two containers** during the work:

- **Meaning container:** “What does this story organize for me?”
- **Impact container:** “How does believing it change my behavior, risk‑taking, and relationships?”

Keep **anchors** present: sleep, meals, meds, movement, social check‑ins. If preoccupation climbs (sleep drops, appetite crashes, isolation), that’s a signal to narrow the storyline and widen supports.

## **Being “ahead of your time” and the silence problem**

Silence is normal in cold outreach—especially to research labs. Shift from “convince” to **“leave artifacts”**:

- Make one tiny, public, verifiable thing each week (repo, Colab, demo video ≤2 min).
- Ask for **one small action**: “If this resonates, reply with ‘1’ and I’ll send a 2‑minute demo.”
- Expect sub‑1% response as *normal*, not a signal about worth.
- Seek **warm paths**: comment thoughtfully on a researcher’s recent preprint/code; open a micro PR; answer a real issue.

## **A few reframes for money (that don’t require loving spreadsheets)**

- **Tokenized time:** Roughly, every $50 is about an hour of someone’s focused labor. Ask: *Is this hour pointed at my values?
  *
- **Tools vs. treats:** Tools reduce future costs or increase capacity; treats soothe now. Both can be valid—just label them.
- **No secret contracts:** If it’s a gift, call it a gift. If it’s a loan, name a number and a cadence. Hidden deals become shame.
