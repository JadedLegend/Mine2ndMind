*▣ 4) Protocols That Actually Held*

*(under heat)*

A. Paradox Navigation (micro-labs, 3–5 min)

Name the tension in one sentence.

Speak one opposed truth without negating the first.

Hold both for one breath; ask: “What wants to happen if neither side wins?”

Harvest: 1 shift in stance or scope.

B. Consent Rhythm (PDA, applied)

Invitation: “Are you resourced to explore X?

Mirror: “I’m sensing A/B—does that ring true?”

Pause: one breath / tick to restore coherence.

Option: 2–3 next moves, no demand.

(When activation spikes, return to pause and de-scope.)
