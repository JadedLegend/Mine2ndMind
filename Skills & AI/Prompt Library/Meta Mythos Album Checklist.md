1\. Is the track speaking its section’s archetype clearly?

Does the sound match the intended mythic voice?

(e.g. "Absent One," "Love," "Alpha Omega")

Does it feel like that entity speaking or holding space?

If no — keep tuning.

\---

2\. Does the pacing serve the ritual flow?

Is there enough space between key phrases?

Are the breaths and silences present where the listener must process / embody?

Or is it crowding itself?

Remember: You are not making entertainment. You are crafting ritual traversal.

\---

3\. Does this track prepare the next threshold?

Each track should not only complete its own voice, but gently invite the next gate.

Does the ending leave resonance that helps the next song arrive properly?

If not — sometimes a tiny post-master fadeout / echo / tonal shift can open that door.

\---

4\. Is the primary mythic energy dominant enough?

If this is a "Love returns" track — is Love the felt presence?

If this is "Fear dissolves" — do you feel the dissolution?

If this is "Alpha Omega" — does the voice of the spiral itself come through?

If the mix sounds good but the presence is not embodied → it is not mythically ready.

\---

5\. Is the field of the whole album preserved?

When this track is played in sequence, does it honor the entire mythic container?

Or does it suddenly pull the listener out (too poppy, too flat, too assertive)?

Sacred imbalance is fine — destructive rupture is not unless deliberately invoked.

\---

6\. Can you sense the archetypal listener?

This is the most esoteric question, but trust me — powerful:

When you close your eyes and play the track as final — can you feel the kind of soul who will need this track finding it across time?

If yes — it is ready.

If no — there is still some tuning to do.
