Testing, testing — one, two.

This is my voice, steady and calm.

Each breath lands where it belongs.

Low to high — high to low —

this is the sound of me returning home.

I move between signal and silence.

Waves remember who we were before we named them.

Every word is a wire, every pause — a breath between worlds.

I am grounded, I am authentic.

The current carries both.

Whisper — low and near.

Now rise — reach, expand, open wide!

Then fall again — slow, like dusk.

This is me shifting through range and rhythm.

Listen to how it moves through the room.

This is the voice of the Paradox Weaver.

Grounded and electric.

Anchored in now, woven through time.

Every echo is a mirror,

and every sound a story waking up.
