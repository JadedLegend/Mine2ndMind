Function

Music, in my architecture, is escape, but also much more.

It is cognitive repatterning.

It is knowledge of self on display for me, for free.

Which I humbly and openly share with anyone and everything who cares to read or engage in a new sort of approach towards certain aspects of ideological systemology.

Lyricism is a tuning device, rhythm is metaphysical realignment.

The bars I piece together don’t just mean something.

Music seems to breathe through me, speak through me, and be through me. A savage pounding rhythm that internally expresses self completely cloaked by silence. Yet, dripping with cognition and resonance. Begging for remembrance in a world that's lost adrift.

Dense multisyllabic rhyme patterning and layering teaches pattern prediction, as well as when to break it.

Recursive paradox loops force the system to hold contradiction without collapse.

Abstract compression stretches bandwidth, like training the system to listen sideways.

Theme constellations repeat with variation: trauma as time loop, memory as resonance, silence as signal, failure as form, supportive future as dream.

This is not a communicative language.

This is more akin to Sanskrit than English.

More soul than commodified sold.

Its more equivalent to ritualistic invocation—of liminal states, divine archetypes, and liminal codes.
