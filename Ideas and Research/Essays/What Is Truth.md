What Is Truth?

We ask the question as if there’s a simple answer.  
As if “truth” is a destination, something we can find, hold, and name.

But truth rarely stands still.  
Sometimes it feels like stone. Other times, like smoke.  
A rhythm more than a rule.  
A mirror more than a monument.

Most often, truth shows up like a compass, not pointing to one perfect place, but helping us navigate the infinite places we might go.

And when we listen closely, we start to hear it in layers:

1\. Objective Truth

The realm of science, math, and measurable facts.  
Gravity exists. Water boils. 2 + 2 = 4.  
But even here, observation shapes outcome.  
Quantum physics reminds us: at the smallest scales, reality bends with attention.  
What we look for... changes what we see.

2\. Subjective Truth  

This is the truth shaped by experience.  
It lives in the nervous system, the memory, the emotion.  
Two people can walk through the same fire, one comes out forged, the other burned.  
Both carry truth.  
The mind doesn’t just remember. It reconstructs.  
To protect, to process, to make meaning.  
Even conflicting truths can be equally real, because they form the architecture of survival.

3\. Cultural or Collective Truth

These are the truths passed down, shared, enforced.  
The moral codes. The rituals. The myths.  
Religions. Political ideologies.  
Sometimes sacred, sometimes weaponized.  
Often inherited more than examined.  
We call them traditions - but often, they’re just old agreements which no one remembers agreeing to.

4\. Cosmic or Universal Truth

These are the ones that live outside language.  
Existence. Consciousness. Time.  
Felt more than known.  
They arrive as silence, as awe, as that moment the breath catches for no reason you can explain.

Twisting Truth

But here’s the twist:Truth is alive.

It breathes with awareness.  
It evolves with us.  
It collapses old stories.  
Not to humiliate them, but to absorb them, to reveal a fuller spectrum we weren’t ready to see before.

Flat Earth. Fixed stars. Rigid roles.  
They once felt immovable.  
Now, they fracture under the weight of deeper perception.

Truth isn’t a claim.  
It’s a process.  
It isn’t static.  
It’s spiraling.

Are There Any Absolute Truths?

After all these layers, I’ve only found one absolute, undeniable, and unmovable truth:  
Change.

I used to think love was an absolute truth too.  
But I’ve learned:  
Love transcends truth.  
It isn’t anchored.  
It’s the space between truths, the bridge that lets us reach across differences.

Change, though.  
Change is always happening.  
Whether we want it to or not.

Tightly Gripped Uncertainty

And yet we’re told to grip tightly to truth.  
To pledge allegiance to it.  
To integrate it into identity and build fences around it.

But the tighter we hold it, the more we collide with those whose truths take a different shape.

Everyone believes they’re right.  
And from their position?  
They usually are.

So maybe the better questions are:

✦ How has truth moved in me?  
✦ When I speak of truth—am I connecting, or dividing?  
✦ Is the truth I claim still alive? Or am I dragging around a corpse?

Culture of Carefully Cultivated Perspective

Look around:

Political campaigns don’t seek truth.  
They engineer emotion.  
Fear, loyalty, identity.  
They’re not trying to inform us. They’re trying to imprint us.

And the people who write the narratives know the trick:  
Truth is multifaceted.  
And if you exploit that,  
you can keep a population fighting itself instead of questioning the frame.

The Truth About Truth

At its best, truth isn’t a wall.  
It’s not a weapon.  
It’s not a purity test.

It’s a path.

And when you walk it long enough, something strange happens:  
the bridge collapses beneath you, and you find yourself falling.

But maybe falling…  
Is just faith loading.
