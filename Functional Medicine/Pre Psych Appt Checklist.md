### **Fluid Tracking Approach**

**1\. Pre-Session “Field Scan”**

- Close your eyes for a minute and notice the **overall tonality** of your system: heavy/light, dense/airy, tight/expansive.
- Observe **energy movement**: is it pulsing, stagnant, chaotic, smooth?
- Note **emotional color**: what feels dominant — anxious, calm, restless, curious?
- Optional: sketch or write a few words, symbols, or colors that represent this state.

**2\. During the Session**

- Instead of trying to quantify, just **notice shifts**: where does energy move, what sensations arise, what emotional tones appear or dissipate?
- Maybe assign **arrows or waves** in your notes rather than numbers — rising, falling, circling, radiating.

**3\. Post-Session Reflection**

- Re-scan your field: what’s different in texture, flow, or color compared to pre-session?
- Highlight moments of resonance: “This area feels lighter,” “My thoughts feel less crowded,” “A wave of calm through the chest.”
- Use metaphor, imagery, or short descriptors rather than numbers.

**4\. Short-Term Flow Notes**

- Track patterns over hours or days: recurring sensations, emotional motifs, energetic shifts.
- Draw lines, shapes, or clusters if that helps capture how things ebb and flow.

**5\. Evaluation Through Pattern Recognition**

- Look for **emergent patterns** rather than isolated metrics: sustained clarity, recurring calm, shifts in density or tension.
- Ask: does this feel like a real shift in your lived experience? Does it resonate with your system?
