(establish GABA tone, smooth ECS, reduce inflammation)

Take daily:

Timing Stack Notes

Omega-3 (2 softgels)

Vitamin D3 + K2

PEA 300 mg

CBD 25 mg

Observe mood, joint comfort, blood-sugar steadiness.

Mid-day

Optional CBD 25 mg if agitation / irritability.

Watch for smoother comedown, less compulsive thinking.

PM with dinner or before bed Magnesium Glycinate

Glycine (3 g powder)

Taurine 500 mg

First nights may bring heavier sleep, vivid dreams, deep sighing.

What to track:

Sleep onset / vividness of dreams

Morning anxiety level (1–10)

Bowel regularity

Body tension (neck / back baseline)

Goal: feel a palpable drop in background noise without collapse of motivation.

(introduce gentle drive and cognitive clarity)

Expected: slight lift in focus, creativity, and curiosity; energy may oscillate—ride it, don’t chase it.

⚖️ Adjustment Guide

Signal Likely Cause Response

Loose stool / fatigue

Too much magnesium or fiber

Halve dose 3 days, hydrate, resume slowly

Jittery focus

Excess Rhodiola

Reduce to half cap AM only

Emotional flattening

CBD dose too high

Drop to half-dropper or pause one day

Vivid dream overload

Glycine > 3 g

Cut dose in half or take earlier evening
