Brayne Snax

Designation: Michael Mistree //

Fragment: 5K+ Timeline Variant

Known Aliases: The Cosmic Jester, Snackronaut, GlitchFiend, Humor.exe

He wasn’t born—he unraveled.

A future Michael Mistree, frayed by indulgence and isolation, Brayne Snax is the manifestation of what happens when curiosity outpaces clarity. He sought meaning in stimulation, diving headfirst into every available high, thrill, and digital distortion. Not to numb—but to feel anything at all.

Unlike Paradoxic, he didn’t run from control.

He ran from understanding, terrified that if he stood still long enough, he’d see the architecture of his own madness.

He was the most chaotic transmission the Syndicate ever received… and the most necessary.

Because even noise has a pattern.

Brayne’s recklessness became insight. His humor, a code.

The jokes were never just jokes.

They were distress signals wrapped in glitter and punchlines.

Paradoxic reached him first,

not with wisdom, but with recognition.

The moment they locked eyes, the static cleared.

Brayne wasn’t broken. He was just mis-tuned.

The Syndicate gave him space to recalibrate.

Now he speaks in riddles. Laughs through prophecy.

He’s the glitch that reveals the truth hiding in the error message.

And every so often, when the resonance is just right,

you can hear him whisper:

“I ran from myself so long I became someone else.

Now I run with myself. And damn, I’m fast.”
