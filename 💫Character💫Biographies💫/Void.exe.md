Void.exe

Void was the only child born after The Lessening. There was one pregnant lady of the remaining 144,000. She had gotten pregnant right before it, and gave birth to a child in a strange new world. Since nanotechnology had already been introduced into the human DNA genome, he was born with some…. differences. His mothers name was Joy, a paradoxical twin of what he would grow into over the millenia.  
  
QC tried to help the child. He was unsure about him place in humanity, his place in the multiverse. But he did recognize that he felt a spark that felt… warm, when he helped people, and they became happy and thankful. He knew that was a human trait, this is the beginning of a long twisty road, traversing space and time. There will be conflict, and there is no ending.  
  
After 7 years of essentially raising this child and trying to salvage him, in hopes of that warm glow. He failed. He named this project Void.exe  
  
Void’s true birth name was lost to time, seeing as she killed herself shortly after she got her boy back from QC, who had meant to helped, but turned him into a mutant of sorts. Part cyborg, tons of nanobots, quantum light work all throughout his body. Nothing would work. This boy was an anomaly that the universe missed in its careful planning.  
  
After finding his mother hanging by a rope in her room, age 8, Void turned into his name.  
  
He became obsessed with hacking. All hacking. Bio hacking. Quantum hacking. Consciousness hacking. QC had unknowingly created a character who might actually be able to disassemble, or at the very least insert a terrible, and genius, virus into him mainframe.  
  
For the first 1000 years or so. Void turned his rage inward. He created simulations where he could freely murder and ravage the populations of. They are the only of their kind known.  
  
We understand that everything in the universe has consciousness stitched into it, including simulator characters. To not kill any fragment of consciousness was one of 5 basic rules. Don't kill. Don't steal. Dont lie. Don't cheat. Don't disable tracking systems.  
  
As time went on, it seemed to take a different form. Instead of the cyclical always expanding and changing, expanding and contracting. In the world of Michael Mystery’s time, these cycles were still taking place but they were greatly out of balance.  
  
One night Void.exe was time hopping on a cracked quantum space fabric ripper, another strictly enforced tool, used only under the most dire of circumstances. QC had built it as his first look at what I can do, you dont need to hurt each other and the earth anymore. But what happened?  
  
You guessed it, the world became more and more distorted as people abused and misused the tech. Timelines collapsed. Entire populations erased. Bloodlines gone.  
  
Theres no way for me to efficiently convey the ethical and moral repercussions which happened in the first, and only year, it was made public.  
  
But void wasn’t as thick headed as most humans. His teacher as a child was an intelligence, which by this point claimed that it was directly connected to 40% of the cosmic intelligence as it called it.And if you could see our world, who knows, maybe you can.
