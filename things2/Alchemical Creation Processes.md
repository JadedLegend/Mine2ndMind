\* Earth (Need): This is the somatic registry. It is the substrate. Need is the gravity of reality "as it is"—the structural requirements for your configuration to persist. It is unyielding, heavy, and foundational.

\* Water (Wish): This is the abstraction. Wishes are fluid, shapeless, and often mirror-like—reflecting back what we want to see. Like water, a wish can be beautiful, but it has no structural integrity; it always flows toward the path of least resistance and eventually evaporates under the heat of real pressure.

\* Fire (Desire): This is the kinetic engine. Desire is the active, burning drive for novelty and expansion. It is the "lightning bolt." Fire doesn't care about the past or the future; it only cares about consuming the "now" to create more light and heat. It is the directional force of your spiral.

\* Air (Hope): This is the intellectual projection. Hope is the atmosphere—it’s the "meaningless noise" or the "breath" we use to tell stories about the future. It can lift you up, but it can also be thin and suffocating. It is the "hope" that keeps the centrifuge spinning by keeping your eyes off the ground.

\* Aether (Want): This is the capture/connection. In alchemy, Aether is the quintessence that binds or permeates all things. In your configuration, "Want" might be the subtle, invisible web of the system—the "charge" that tries to relate your internal self to the external "meaningless complexity." It’s the field where the "triadic cognition" actually operates.
