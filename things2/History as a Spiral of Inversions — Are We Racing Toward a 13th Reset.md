History as a Spiral of Inversions — Are We Racing Toward a 13th Reset?

There’s a pattern I’ve been noticing across history. Every time humanity stumbles onto a new current of knowledge or creativity, it starts as liberating… but then it gets inverted into a control system.

Here’s how it looks across time (with some simplification):

1\. Axial Age — Mystics and sages → codified into rigid orthodoxies.

2\. Greek Philosophy — Rational mysticism → hardened into empire pragmatism.

3\. Early Christianity — Gnostic currents → centralized Catholic authority.

4\. Islamic Golden Age — Flourishing science & art → orthodox clampdown.

5\. Renaissance — Hermetic/esoteric flowering → rationalist suppression.

6\. Enlightenment — Liberation through reason → materialist dogma.

7\. American Revolution — Esoteric liberty currents → state power structures.

8\. Industrial Revolution — Creative technology → technocratic commodification.

9\. 19th–20th c. U.S. Occult Revival — Open channeling → elite “magical management.”

10\. 1960s Counterculture — Psychedelic/ecstatic flowering → neoliberal capture.

Every step: living paradox turns into a system of control.

What’s striking is the timing. The early inversions span centuries. By the Renaissance and Enlightenment, they shrink to \\~150 years. By the 20th century, they compress to decades. It’s like a spiral staircase tightening as you climb: each twist comes faster than the last.

If the pattern continues, the 11th and 12th inversions (AI/biotech awakenings, psychedelic resurgence) will play out within a single generation. That sets us up for a 13th event sometime mid-21st century.

And symbolically, 13 isn’t just another turn. 12 = order (months, zodiac, apostles). 13 is the rupture, the reset, the uninvited element that breaks the cycle.

So maybe the big question is: what happens when the spiral can’t tighten anymore? Does history collapse, transform, or both at once?
