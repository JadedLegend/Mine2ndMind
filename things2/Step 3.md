Peaceful Excavation of identities

Identity appears to be the form(s) through which a person-pattern becomes recognizable.

Self may be the continuity that remains in relationship beneath those forms.

Identities seem sort of like an assorted assortment of costumes.

Some we pick.

Some are chosen for us.

Some can be changed.

Some must remain.

They appear in nearly every imaginable configuration.

Beneath them - appears something lying. 😉

Repeating archetypal patterns.

Each with a spectrum of... stuff available to them.

These patterns of sorts seem to largely define the way in which we choose to experience one another, ourselves, and the world surrounding.

Identity is not one thing.

How could it be? We have discovered that nothing is one thing, through our scientific method no less.

Identity - itself - almost appears to have a driven desire to be identified as just one.

Which opens into a whole new line of inquiry, which doesn't seem useful here.

Identity could very well be one of the fundamental and foundational pillars of what it actually means to be Human.

Self discovery may be - referring to just that.

You and the body you inhabit. Here. Now.

Was not an ancient Atlantian Priestess.

Was not a mystic Peruvian Shaman.

Was not a starseed planted by Pleiadians.

You... are you.

Is it a possibility something similar to this may be true, some place, some time, some where, some how?

Sure. Absolutely.

At the same time - try and recognize it may just as likely be symbolic compression allowed to decompress.

Or it could be schizophrenia, aliens, collective thought-forms.

Who truly knows? Surely not me.

All that can ever really be truly known.

Is your experience and relationship to it.

The inner experience and outer experience may rhyme.

The inner and outer experience may influence one another.

The inner/outer may have been referring to an aspect of existence, which is relational in nature.

The word "identity" ultimately derives from the Latin pronoun idem, meaning "the same".

Every identity you wear, has the same you wearing it.

Who you are becomes changed by the identity it wears.

A healthy identity excavation looks like integration of valuable aspects, and conscious disregard and release of damaging aspects.

Unhealthy identity work can not be called excavation.

We appear to be relational beings.

Life appears almost entirely as a relational process.

Identity seems necessary for the continuity of our collective existence.

Some questions, maybe worth asking.

Who do I identify as? Why do I identify as that?

The mind normally appears to default toward grasping — sometimes desperately — for people, concepts, symbols, places, and so on and such.

Perhaps because identity, continuity, and relational orientation are not fixed structures, but unstable fields seeking temporary form.

It's quite often understandably disorienting.

It's quite often awkward and disquieting.

It's quite often outside the register of language.

Identities dissolving may disturb dormant danger.

Danger is like most all things.

Deserving closer inspection.

Often best left alone.

What might remain - individually - were identity to be recognized as a temporary fixture?

What if identities were seen as attempts to find a sense of equilibrium and stability atop an omnipresent undercurrent?

This staircase - being desgined - as spiral and such.

Perhaps does not have one proper entry point.

Perhaps intuition and discernment are the more useful 'tools' here.

Identity identification, and missing.

Which is a natural part of the process.

Holds dormant potential to lead into a host of emotional, physical, and mental states.

Some nice, others not as much.

The entirety of this framework is completely dependent on a radical, individuated willingness - aimed roughly towards developing a perpetuating relationship with self responsibility.

This does not excuse perpetrators.

It may allow access to new perspective.

A point of view which isn't quite so abrasive.

The pathway placement of identity identification is best approached with deliberate intention.

First - identify the identity.

Next - begin the process of excavation

Finally - reassembly.

This is how human development appears to work most effectively, through a recursive process.

The states of mind which I associate most closely with identity association/disassociation and attachment/release - would likely be - frustration, jubilation, and emancipation.

Processes processing do not appear linear. They appear spiraling.

Processing processes beginning through finishing.

Lived relation makes the nascent lost its patience.
