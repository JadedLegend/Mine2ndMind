# *Pitch - Why This Idea May Work*

**Three-Control Trash Can Lid — Working Pitch**

Most trash cans force the user into one control method: step on the pedal, wave at a sensor, or touch the lid. Each method has friction. Pedals require foot positioning. Motion sensors guess intent and can open or close at the wrong time. Touching the lid is dirty and inconvenient.

This idea is a trash can lid with three deliberate controls:

1. A bottom foot pedal.
2. A button on the can.
3. An external stick-on button that can be mounted nearby.

The two buttons work as toggles: press once to open, press again to close.

The product is not trying to be a “smart trash can.” It is trying to be an obedient trash can. No motion guessing. No timer closing before the user is finished. No app required. The lid opens when commanded and closes when commanded.

The core value is simple: put lid control where the user actually is. A person cooking, cleaning, carrying trash, working at a counter, or moving around a kitchen should not have to reposition their body just to open a can.

The first prototype should prove one thing: three intentional inputs can control one lid reliably. If that works, the idea becomes easy to understand, easy to demonstrate, and easy to improve.
