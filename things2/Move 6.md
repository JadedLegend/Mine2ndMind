Communicating Boundaries for Relationships

Once step 3 is grasped - in a somatic manner.

Move 6 can be entered into - with a responsible manner.

3 is primarily about identity/self and our relationship to those aspects of our being.

6 expands the relational field by entertaining the possibility of developing more authentic and substantial relationships with others.

This isn't to say you have a problem.

This isn't to say this is the way for you.

This is simply a gentle offer.

A transmission of what I think has helped me.

In the hopes it may help another person.

Relationships with others may in fact be the most complex facet of the human experience.

Companionship

Friendship

Kinship

Partnership

Courtship

The shipping aspect attached to this concept becomes hard to miss.

Once it's identified in a meaningful way.

Lightbulbs have been known to light up.

We do not enter all relationships the same way.

Some arrive as companions.

Some become friends.

Some are inherited kin.

Some are chosen as partner.

Some begin in courtship.

Some become fellowship, stewardship, or guardianship.

Most all relationships end up tangled, fused, and/or knotted - at some point or another.

Each relationship appears a ship of relation.

One ship may think themselves searching for one thing, the other may think themselves searching for something entirely different.

Sometimes people searching for different things are able to meet - in between.

More often than not, ships appear searching for a multitude of relations.

A friendship needs trust, humor, honesty, and time.

A kinship has history, obligation, forgiveness, and boundary.

A partnership carries mutual responsibility, shared perspective, and perpetual repair.

A courtship may need curiosity, pacing, consent, and restraint.

Through identifying ourselves. We begin to uncover insecurities, fears, and hidden facets. While remaining aware that there is still much unspoken within us.

We might have had the realization that many of our relational conflicts - in the past - may in fact have been more about us then they are the other person.

This is no excuse for any form of wrongdoing.

It's a more accurate weighting of perceived wrongdoing.

Identity work is destabilizing.

It often includes long periods of isolation.

It often includes surprising trials and tribulations.

There is a light at the end of this tunnel.

Relationship to others.

This is something of much importance.

Made to feel tiny and meaningless.

The hinge in relationship is communication.

Communication starts and ends relationships.

While concurrently carrying everything in between.
