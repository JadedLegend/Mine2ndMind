If something is free, \*you\* are the product.

Your data, your preferences, your time, and even pieces of your soul—packaged and sold to the highest bidder.

This is the reality we all inhabit, whether we like it or not. Though we may each view it through our own lens, we all live in the same interconnected system. Many have tried to unplug, to step away from a world increasingly dominated by technology and the internet, but escaping it entirely seems nearly impossible.

Somewhere along the way, we collectively decided that the best use of the internet—a tool of infinite potential—was to advance agendas of marketing and manipulation. Instead of realizing the promise of this generation-defining technology, we've squandered its possibilities. What could have been a unifying force has become a platform for control, division, and exploitation.

The way the internet was created and distributed reflects an astonishing lack of foresight—a system built on a foundation that is unintelligent and, ultimately, self-defeating.

Marketing and manipulation. Is this truly the best we can do?
