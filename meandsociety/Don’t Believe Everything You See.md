Don’t Believe Everything You See

Just because something shows up repeatedly on your newsfeed doesn’t mean it’s true. In a world overwhelmed by opinions, algorithms, and biased narratives, it’s more important than ever to think critically.

Here’s a simple guide to navigating the noise:

1\. Research Both Ends of the Spectrum

Don’t settle for just one perspective. Seek out multiple points of view, even those that challenge your personal beliefs.

2\. Cross-Reference Your Information

Verify what you’re reading. If multiple credible sources back it up, it holds more weight.

3\. Articulate Your Beliefs in Your Own Words

Once you’ve reached a decision, make sure you truly understand it. Being able to explain your perspective clearly shows you’ve done the work.

4\. Stay Open-Minded

Paradigm shifts aren’t easy, but they’re necessary for growth. Be willing to question everything—even your most cherished beliefs.

Remember, cognitive dissonance goes both ways. Being a truth seeker doesn’t mean automatically dismissing mainstream ideas, nor does it mean blindly believing alternative narratives. It means actively searching for what resonates as true and staying adaptable as new information comes to light.
