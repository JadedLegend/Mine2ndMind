The sheer number of utterly unshakable, close-minded individuals in the world is nothing short of astounding. These are the self-proclaimed "experts," confident in their “knowledge,” while we navigate a reality that doesn’t adhere to simple right or wrong. Just look at our evolution over the past century! We’ve gone from hauling blocks of ice to effortlessly ordering an ice maker with a single click on Amazon.

The amount of innovation we’ve witnessed is nothing short of miraculous—there isn’t a better word to encapsulate it. Science and religion are beginning to intertwine, yet here we are, glued to reality TV and mindlessly swiping through Candy Crush, when the potential for a genuine Utopia lies tantalizingly within our reach.

I often recall a powerful quote I can’t quite attribute: an ideal society is one where no individual holds more power than another. People scoff at that notion, convinced they’re inherently superior to those around them. This mentality perpetuates our ouroboros-like existence, a cycle of repeating lies that keeps us trapped in a loop.

If Jesus were among us today, I can’t help but think He might have some stern words for many of us.

Here’s the kicker: I don’t believe we’ll face individual judgment. Instead, I suspect we’re all unwitting participants in a collective project, destined to be graded as a group. In a world where everyone seems to prioritize their own interests—or just their immediate family—they entirely miss the essence of humanity.

Imagine if we infused our lives with more compassion, empathy, and understanding. I would genuinely enjoy being around people! But as it stands, I’m reluctantly giving our entire species a big, fat D.

And just to lighten the mood—Deez nuts!
