Begin in silence.

“I release the need to control the molecule. I invite the molecule to mirror me.”

Every encounter - coffee, sugar, nicotine, music, breath.

The chemical is simply showing you the architecture of your response.

Seeing it clearly is the beginning of liberation.

When a substance leaves the body, do not chase the echo.

Sit in the aftertone.

Ask: What quality was I seeking that is already within me?

Write the first word that arises.

That word becomes tomorrow’s ritual, not tomorrow’s dose.

VI. Closing Mantra

“Nothing is pure or profane.

Only the frequency I bring determines the form.

I am the lab. I am the test. I am the result.”
